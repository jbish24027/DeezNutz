## 1. Made in-house — send these files

These two do not exist on Nexus. Your friend cannot download them.

| Mod folder | Size | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Authoria - 3DNPC Merge` | 1.2 GB | The faces. Baked head meshes and textures for every Interesting NPC, picked from the appearance replacers below. |
| `houseCARL - Authoria - Patch - Requiem - Interesting NPCs` | 270 KB | The Requiem balance pass. Levels, perks, factions, gear for the Interesting NPCs cast. |

Masters: Skyrim, 3DNPC, Project ja-Kha'jay Khajiit Diversity, Requiem,
Requiem - Weapons and Armor Redone, Requiem - Magic Redone.

### What the Requiem patch needs on the other end
Masters: Skyrim, Update, HearthFires, 3DNPC, RSkyrimChildren, AI Overhaul,
Project ja-Kha'jay Khajiit Diversity, leyrur daelkhun Luaffyn WeelBones Replacer,
3DNPCs_Reimagined, JKJ - 3DNPC, Cuyima 3DNPC - Redone, Requiem,
Requiem - Food and Beverages Redone, DBM_3DNPC_Patch.

If any of those are missing the plugin will not load.

## 3. Do NOT send

These also touch Interesting NPCs, but they are generated on this machine
and go stale the moment your friend re-runs the tool:

- `Authoria - RFTI Output` — Reqtificator output. Your friend runs their own.
- `Authoria - Synthesis Output - NSFW` — Synthesis output, gameplay and worldspace. Same.

## Install order on the other end

Keep the same relative order as here:

1. Interesting NPCs (3DNPC), then the 4.54 update over it.
2. The Nexus fixes, replacers and addons.
3. `Authoria - 3DNPC Merge` — must win over every appearance replacer above.
4. `houseCARL - Authoria - Patch - Requiem - Interesting NPCs` — plugin loads after Requiem
   and after DBM_3DNPC_Patch.
5. Re-run Reqtificator and Synthesis last.
