### Notes on this group

- Nine of the sixteen are voiced by the person who built the mod.
- Ashe and Astrid are the two with hired professional cast. Ashe's supporting cast includes Sean Chiplock.
- Kaidan's mod also credits Tanya S Bartlett and LivTempleton for other characters in that mod.
- M'rissi's installer offered two voice actresses. The FOMOD choice recorded in the mod is Sininensusi.
  The alternative, AdloraHealer, is not installed.
- S'ahara is the housemaid from M'rissi's mod. She is registered as a follower by the Requiem bridge script.
- Auri's audio runs through "Enhanced Voice Remastered" by TheVoidKnight. That is a cleanup pass on
  Waribiki's original recordings, not a re-performance.
- Thogra is on the Revoiced version. The old legacy version used vanilla lines plus SKVA and is not installed.
- Yoana is tagged AI-Generated Content on Nexus. Treat the voice as synthetic.
- Kaidan - Immersive Features is a large off-Nexus community add-on that adds further Kaidan lines.
  Its voice credits were not confirmed during this scan.

## 2. The big-cast mod

Interesting NPCs (3DNPC), Nexus 29194, is installed and enabled.
It adds dozens of recruitable followers on its own, including Zora Fair-Child, Rumarin, Anum-La and Amalee.
It uses a volunteer cast of roughly 250 voice actors, so there is no single name to give.
The full cast is listed in the mod's own credits.
