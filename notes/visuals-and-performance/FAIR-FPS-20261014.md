# Wanderer's Fair fps - findings 2026-10-14

Question: "FPS in Wanderer's Fair is awful. Do we need to cache grass?"
Method: 5 read-only investigators (grass, crowd, per-NPC cost, logs, render load), each re-checked by a skeptic.
Raw results: session scratchpad `35577e0a-...\tasks\wlkauwlx7.output` and `scratchpad\digest.txt`.

## Grass: NOT the cause
- `GrassControl.ini` (only copy: `mods\Authoria - MCM and INI Settings\SKSE\Plugins`) has Use-grass-cache=true and Only-load-from-cache=true.
- No `SkyrimFairWorld*.cgid` exists in any enabled mod, BSA, overwrite or Stock Game. Grass Cache Helper turns runtime creation off.
- So the fair draws no grass at all. Grass costs 0 ms there.
- The fair's land is ~98.6% grass-bearing tundra (Witchy Wilderness LTundra01), so it looks bare without a cache.
- A cache adds grass and some cost; it can't raise fps. SkyrimFairWorld is already in Only-pregenerate-world-spaces, so the owed
  full grass regen ([[grass-cache-july-stale-regen-needed]]) will include it.
- Side risk: Updating-Cache=true lets NGIO switch creation/writing on at startup; only Grass Cache Helper's later write turns it off.
  Harmless today; flip to false outside regen jobs.

## What does cost fps at the fair
1. **Crowd size.** 247 placed actors. 36 start disabled and stay off. 27 are never culled (band, orchestra, singers, archers, horses).
   184 are run by SkyrimFairCrowdCull. Walk With Me logged 211 high-process actors on arrival on all three visits
   (Oct 1 x2, today), 142 after the cull, vs 48-80 in Solitude and 42-50 in Whiterun hold / Falkreath.
   The cull is a Papyrus poll (0.5 s at the fair) so it does nothing while paused: the after-load pause keeps all 211 on.
2. **Dense scene.** ~2,780 visible objects in four cells (cell 0,0 alone ~900), about twice Riverwood's 3x3. ~6.9M static triangles.
   196 paper lanterns are 23% of the static triangles (6.6-9.5k tris each, all pieces cast shadows). 220 animated NotWL pines.
3. **Heavy characters.** 3BA outfits measure 80-128k skinned triangles each. ~62-83% of visitors get SMP hair (Vanilla hair remake SMP),
   ~22 wear SMP clothing. FSMP is capped (5 skeletons, 3 ms) and CBPC is capped (50 actors, 1024 u, 120 deg), so physics is bounded.
   Shadows: fShadowDistance 10000 covers the whole fair.
4. **Per-NPC scripts still reaching fair NPCs** (mostly arrival script lag, small steady fps): Furniture Height Size Fix cloak
   (64 actors x up to 6 starts at arrival, re-fires when the cull re-enables actors), Requiem race armor-weight script,
   Wet and Cold `_WetControlSpell` (no -SkyrimFairNPC; gives cold gear to every visitor within 6144 u in snow or months 0/1/11),
   IEA (cheap for non-followers), Sekiro, SAO, Use Those Blankets, Vanilla Eating Animation Fix, OISeeYouMasturbating (10 s scan).
   The seven existing -SkyrimFairNPC lines are intact; the reseat loop is gone (No Reseat patch works).
