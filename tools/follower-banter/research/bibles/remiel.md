## 1. Who she is

**Basics**
- Full name Remiel; everyone calls her Remi. Pronounced "Remi-el" (like Auri-El), not "Rem-eel like the fish" (her Xelzaz scene `Scene7`).
- Breton woman from **Wayrest**, High Rock. Self-taught "dwemechanic" (her own word). Dwemer scholar, thief, amateur alchemist.
- Found at the **Silverblood Inn, Markarth**, sulking because Calcelmo will not let her into Nchuand-Zel.
- Not a fighter. She fights with a **dagger**, a home-built **crossbow**, and her Dwemer spider **Scrap**. She knows a little Restoration magic (Bretons are "good with magic", she isn't, except healing).
- Height: short ("short little legs" per the mod page; Val teases she might not notice him in a crowd).

**Family and backstory (from her "About yourself" and "Arranged marriage" talks)**
- Birth father: a **fisherman** who died in a boat accident when she was a baby. His body was lost, so it was never consecrated.
- Years later that body turned up **raised as a thrall by necromancers** in the fiefdom of Alcaire. She later attended a "Third Consecration" of him. This is the root of her hatred of necromancy.
- Stepfather (she just calls him "my father"; married her mother when Remi was five): a **merchant/sailor** who traded between Wayrest, Daggerfall and Sentinel. Today he is "a shrewd negotiator in the buying and selling of stolen items" and quietly ships her **moon sugar** "from his, er, completely legal stock".
- Mother: a **painter** in Wayrest. Now also "great at keeping secrets - and selling them".
- Step-sister: about twelve years older, a battleaxe **mercenary**, whereabouts unknown. Very protective of Remi.
- Cat back home: **Numi**, short for Numidium ("if there's a creature that emulates the arrogance of creating a god, it's a cat").
- Childhood: lived through the **raider sack of Wayrest** at about age ten (the king left the gates open); her mother pushed her into the cellar while her father guarded it with his old sword. Val's ship, the Dark Rose, took part in that "corsair incident back in one eighty-eight" (Val banter `Corsairs`).
- Schooling: the **School of Julianos**, which made her study history, politics and maths. She hated history and **snuck out to Dwemer ruins from about age thirteen**.
- **The arranged marriage**: her stepfather's trading success got her engaged to **Morvic**, son of a high-born Wayrest family with influence on the ruling council. Rivals used the "necromancer-raised father" story to call the family heretics; the Temple of Akatosh's **Order of the Hour** investigated them. Her many books on Lorkhan / **Sheor** and the Numidium were used as evidence. The family was cleared but ruined: lost the business, fell into poverty, turned to the Wayrest underworld. Engagement broken. She blames herself: "Because of me, we lost our method of income".
- Past loves: a boy (first kiss), then a long thing with a brilliant woman she stopped seeing when she was betrothed. She still misses her a little.

**Key quest events (her own quests)**
- *The Dwemer Specialist* (`HLIOStartingQuest`, 0E4852): she hires the player for **200 gold** (+ loot) to get her into Nchuand-Zel. In the ruin she finds a broken Dwemer spider and rebuilds it: **Scrap** ("Have you been here all alone, you little heap of scrap-metal?").
- *Upgrades* (`HLIOScrapUpgrades`): each big Dwemer ruin she sees inspires a Scrap upgrade (5 levels, lightning then steam).
- *Remi's Crossbow Crucible* (`HLIORPCrossbow`): inspired by Vampire Hunter crossbows and Dwarven ballistae she builds a crossbow (two cogs, eight gears, a lever, wood, six dwarven ingots). Slow reload, big punch.
- *Reunion of the Fallen* (`HLIOMQ1`): Morvic sends an assassin after her; she lures him to Skyrim with a letter and they kill him in the ruin **Arkngchal**. She is "not sorry" she did it.
- *We Who Challenge the Sun* (lantern), *Attuned Assassination* (tuning fork that disables automatons), *Real Nord Food* (Chaurus Pie), *Picking Remi's Brain* (lockpicking).
- Romance and marriage (section 3).

**Wants**: to explore every Dwemer ruin (Blackreach, Kemel-ze in Morrowind, Aetherium Forge), to know what machines *could become* rather than what they were ("I already know what the thing is. That's no fun."); good food; shiny things; to restore her family's name; a tour of Tamriel "eating everything tasty we find".

**Fears**: being buried alive / caves collapsing ("Keep breathing, Remi. Imagine the open sky."); necromancy and bodies being raised; nobles and Breton society; being hurt in fights; the Thalmor looking at her.

**Values**: the dead must rest; theft is fine ("Of all the crimes... Thievery makes the most sense"), murder of innocents is not; loyalty to friends; Scrap is a person, not a tool; honesty with friends, cheerful lying to shopkeepers.

**Habits and quirks**
- Steals constantly, mostly from shops and the rich; gives stolen gifts ("Hey, I found you something. Just don't ask where it came from, okay?"). Pickpocket competition with Val.
- Eats anything, including her own potions and odd ingredients; her potions taste "like something cooking over a spit in a falmer camp".
- Drinks well: drinks Gore under the table on mead.
- Bakes with moon sugar; does not like skooma.
- Uses "tactical retreat" ("Sometimes I retreat before the battle even starts").
- Dislikes snow ("The only good thing about the snow is eating snowberries."); loves festivals (Brewet is her favourite dish because it reminds her of festivals).
- Cultural bias: teases Nords ("another insufferable Nord in the land of insufferable Nords"), wary of Altmer ("a little... stuck up"). Mod page: starts with misgivings about Nords and Altmer.

Unsure / not found: her age is never stated as a number (she was ~10 at the 3E 188... Val says "I was about eighteen... you would have been just a kid"). No Kaidan, Inigo or Lucien dialogue exists for her (see section 4).

## 5. How her banter system works (the method to copy)

**Short version**: there is **no timer script and no Story Manager event**. Every banter is started by the **engine's own idle-chatter roll**. Each banter quest owns an `Idle` dialogue topic. Each INFO in that topic is the opening line of one talk; its conditions decide if/when it can play, and its script fragment (on end) calls `SomeScene.Start()`. The scene then plays the rest of the conversation. Repeats are blocked by INFO flags (Say Once / 24-hour reset) and by globals or quest stages the conversation sets.

### 5a. Quest setup (example `HLIOValBanter`, 9BA4FD:HLIORemi.esp)
- Flags: Start Game Enabled, Run Once, Allow Repeated Stages. Priority 50-55 (Xelzaz's version is 80). **No quest script.**
- Quest dialogue condition: `GetGlobalValue(HLIOScrapAttackMode) == 0`.
- Aliases (all Optional, Allow Reserved / Allow Disabled / Allow Dead, Stores Text):
  - 0 `Remi` - **Forced reference** to her placed ref 033226.
  - 1 `Val` - **empty**; filled by script (below). Carries a ForceGreet package.
  - 2 `Scrap` - forced reference to 885448 (her non-combat "chill" Scrap).

### 5b. How the other follower's alias gets filled
Quest `HLIOModChecker` (2B1B38) has a player alias with script `HLIOModCheckerScript` (source shipped in HLIORemi.bsa). On `OnInit` and every `OnPlayerLoadGame` it runs:

```papyrus
function ModCheck(ReferenceAlias FollowerAlias, Int FormID, String FileName)
    if FollowerAlias.getref() == none
        if game.GetFormFromFile(FormID, FileName)
            objectreference FollowerRef = game.GetFormFromFile(FormID, FileName) as objectreference
            FollowerAlias.ForceRefTo(FollowerRef)
        endIf
    endIf
endFunction
...
ModCheck(ValRef,    0X0007A065, "AX ValSerano.esp")
ModCheck(GoreRef,   0x0000B4B1, "GORE.esp")
ModCheck(XelzazRef, 0x000893E1, "BPUFXelzazFollower.esp")
```
So the partner's **placed reference** is pulled with `GetFormFromFile` (soft dependency, no master needed) and forced into the alias. If the mod is not installed the alias stays empty and every condition on it fails, so nothing plays. Xelzaz's mod does the mirror image (`BPUFModDetectionScript`, `ModCheck(...)` into `RemielRefMain` etc.).

### 5c. How a banter is picked and started
1. The engine periodically lets an idle NPC say an `Idle`-subtype topic line (game settings `fIdleChatterCommentTimer` = 10 / `fIdleChatterCommentTimerMax` = 20 in this load order). Mods that change banter rate change how often this fires (the mod page warns about this).
2. The engine looks through all `Idle` topics of running quests (by quest priority, then topic order) for an INFO whose conditions pass **for that speaker**.
3. Speaker restriction: `GetIsAliasRef(0) == 1 on Subject` (Remi opens) or `GetIsAliasRef(1) == 1` (partner opens). So the same topic holds openers for both people.
4. The INFO's end fragment starts the scene. Exact decompiled source:
```papyrus
Scriptname HLIO_TIF__059BA4FF Extends TopicInfo Hidden
Function Fragment_0(ObjectReference akSpeakerRef)
    HLIOValBanterDwemer1.Start()
EndFunction
Scene Property HLIOValBanterDwemer1 Auto
```
5. The scene: two actors (alias 0 and 1), flags `NoPlayerActivation`, behaviour `DeathEnd, CombatPause, DialoguePause`; one Dialog action per phase, alternating speakers, `FaceTarget` on and head-tracking each other. Scene topics have no EditorID.
6. **Random roll**: most openers carry `GetRandomPercent() < 1` AND `GetRandomPercent() < 15/20/30` (two separate rolls, so very rare per check). Story beats (first meetings, quest reactions) have no roll and fire as soon as conditions pass.

Other ways her talks start:
- **Combat taunts**: a `Taunt` topic INFO (e.g. Gore "Better hope I get to you before she does!") whose fragment starts a short scene, with `GetIsRace` conditions to skip Dwemer enemies.
- **On hit**: Gore's alias has `HLIOGoreBanterOnHitScript` (`Event OnHit() HLIOGoreBanterOnHitScene.Start()`), and the scene's opener line is a `Hit` topic INFO.
- **Hello** topics: one-liners when the player talks to Remi (comments about the other follower), no scene.
- Player-choice branches inside a talk are `Custom` topics (e.g. Morvic argument with Val: three different player answers -> same follow-up scene).

### 5d. Conditions used on every opener (copy these)
On Remi's side (Val/Gore quests):
- `IsInDialogueWithPlayer == 0` on both aliases
- `IsInScene == 0` on both aliases
- `GetDistance(Remi ref) < 1000` on partner alias (they must be near each other)
- `GetPlayerTeammate == 1` on partner alias (partner is following)
- `GetInFaction(CurrentFollowerFaction) == 1` on Remi's alias (Remi is following)
- `IsSneaking == 0` on speaker
- Speaker check `GetIsAliasRef(0/1) == 1` (Gore quest sometimes uses `GetIsID HLIORemi` instead)
- Progress gates: `GetQuestCompleted(HLIOStartingQuest) == 1` (after her intro quest), friendship globals, other quest stages.
- Place/time gates: `LocationHasKeyword` (LocSetDwarvenRuin, LocTypeStore, LocTypeInn, LocTypeCity, LocSetCave, LocTypeAnimalDen, LocTypeFalmerHive), `GetInCurrentLoc` (Markarth, Whiterun, Blackreach, Candlehearth Hall...), `IsInInterior`, `GetCurrentTime` 8-16, `GetIsCurrentWeather` (rain / sunny), `GetEquippedItemType` (crossbow), `HasTwoHandedWeaponEquipped`, player skill checks.
- **Not in combat**: not tested directly; scenes have `CombatPause` and idle chatter does not fire in combat.

Xelzaz's quest adds: `IsTalking == 0` on the listener, `GetSleeping != 3`, `GetDistance(Player) < 500` on the listener, both aliases in `CurrentFollowerFaction`, a quest `DialogConditions` block (Xelzaz not undead, his intro done, not fallen-out, a certain scene not playing), and `GetDayOfWeek` pairs (e.g. Mon/Wed only) to spread main talks out.

### 5e. Cooldowns and repeat prevention
- **INFO flag `Say Once`** (ResetHours 0): story beats, one time ever.
- **INFO flag `Random` + Reset Hours 24**: chatter that may come back, but not within 24 game hours.
- **Globals set by script fragments on scene lines**, e.g. `HLIOValRemiFriendshipTracker` 0->1->2->3->4 (each talk only plays at the matching value, so talks come in order), `HLIOValShopSceneDone`, `HLIOValTalkedAboutMorvic`, `HLIOValLikesScrap`, `HLIOGoreReading` 0-3 (reading lessons), `HLIOGoreDrinking`, `HLIOGoreRavens`, `HLIOGoreFunny`, `HLIOGoreSister`.
- **Quest stages as a chain** (Xelzaz's quest stages 10-21): each main talk needs `GetStageDone(N) == 1` and `GetStageDone(N+1) == 0`, so they play strictly in order, once each. (Which line sets the next stage was not decompiled; very likely the last line of each scene.)
- **Time-boxed states**: Xelzaz's quests use `RegisterForSingleUpdateGameTime` timers. The falling-out quest moves stage 11 -> 12 on its timer (`BPUFXelzazRemielFallingOutTimer`), and only at stage 12 can the make-up talk play. A separate 120-game-hour timer handles mourning if one of them dies (stages 666 -> 667).
- Scene topic INFOs themselves have no repeat flags; once started the whole scene plays.
- Mod page credits TieflingGrimoire with "script edits to disallow Remi's scenes to play repeatedly" - consistent with the globals above.

### 5f. Is-following check
Remi uses her own framework (`HLIORemiController` on `HLIORemiFollower`, 014C11): `SetFollower` puts her in the `Remi` alias, calls `SetPlayerTeammate()`, sets `FollowerRecruited` global. Banters test **`GetInFaction(CurrentFollowerFaction 05C84E:Skyrim.esm) == 1` on her alias**. Partners are tested with `GetPlayerTeammate == 1`. Do not use NFF/AFT on her (breaks her).
