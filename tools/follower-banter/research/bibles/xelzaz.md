Source: read-only scan of `BPUFXelzazFollower.esp` (+ Wyrmstooth, Sirenroot, SGT Bard Addon, JK New Gnisis) in profile "DEEZ CS NUTZ - PICTA", 2026-10-18. ## 1. Who he is

**Basics**
- Argonian man, **30 years old** ("I am currently thirty years of age").
- Rank: **Lawman of House Telvanni**. He introduces himself as "Xelzaz, Lawman of House Telvanni" almost every time.
- His name is Jel for **"Sanctified Root"**. He doesn't know why his parents chose it, and hopes it "will become clear to me" (from the Khash name quest).
- He is an alchemist by trade and a serious cook. He fights as a spellsword: two daggers, destruction, conjuration/necromancy, and a steady supply of potions.

**Origin and family** (Thorn, Black Marsh)
- **Mother: Shana-Jee.** An egg-tender for the tribe: keeps the egg records and birthsigns, sings to the eggs, and fights off predators. "She's taught me much of what I know" about fighting.
- **Father: Tsuzo-Kai.** A root-herald under the tribe's tree-minder (diplomat, scout and spy). Very devout. He made the deal with Veyla Oren that sent Xelzaz to the Telvanni.
- **Younger sister: Deekawo.** She hatched days before he left. She is quiet and talks almost only through the Hist-bond, which she is unusually good at. He expects her to become a sap-speaker. "We communicate with each other every day."
- He was given a **Hist leaf preserved in amber sap** at his leaving ceremony. He still carries it (the HistLeaf alias and the "Lost and Found" quest).

**Telvanni history**
- He was spotted young as an alchemy prodigy and hired out as a **hireling** to **Veyla Oren**, tower alchemist of **Tel Sul** in Llothanis (north-east Morrowind). This was "at almost the same age as I could walk and talk".
- **He was not a slave.** He says so to the player and to Remiel more than once. He admits the pay made it close: "with the wages I got I might as well have been." He frames it as meritocracy that lifted him, and he is grateful: "They gave a deek-Saxhleel like me a chance."
- He rose under **Master Relivan Sul**. Sul "initially cared little for me" and in the end clearly meant to make Xelzaz his **Mouth**, after the current Mouth **Nalis Faveran** moved up to Spellwright.
- **Nalis Faveran** is his enemy. Xelzaz calls him a flatterer and "a proper snake" who resented him for Sul's favour and for being an Argonian. Xelzaz suspects, without proof, that Nalis paid for Sul's murder. He has vowed: "He only bought himself a few years of safety, before I orchestrate his ultimate demise and humiliation."
- **Sul was murdered by the Morag Tong** on 28 Evening Star, 4E 195. Xelzaz came home from a Black Marsh expedition and found the body, throat torn, with the Tong's writ on the desk. He tells this as a full monologue ("It broke me..."). This is the root of his hatred of paid assassins.
- After that he moved to **Port Telvannis**, did freelance work, and has no Master. Veyla, who he still writes to, sent him to **Helseth Ren**, a Telvanni Master digging at the Doomcrag in Rivenspire who is said not to be prejudiced against non-Dunmer. Xelzaz wants to become **Ren's Mouth**.
- He is walking from Port Telvannis to High Rock **on foot** "to actually see Tamriel" and do field study. The player is hired to escort him to the Hammerfell Gate. He pays them in meals and potions.

**Love: Seryne** (backstory, not the player)
- When he was a teenager in Ranyon-ruhn he met **Seryne**, a Dunmer retainer and aspiring mycologist. Ashen hair; one red eye and one purple; a black crescent mark under her left eye. They became secret lovers for years.
- She was made a Master's Mouth and moved to Port Telvannis, so they ended it to protect each other. She gave him the **red scarf with Telvanni insignia** (his scarf item). He gave her one of his **heart scales**: "I feel certain I can feel her ephemeral heart beat against my own... Even now."
- This tears him in two. The Hist and his family want him to have Argonian children. "My heart cries out for Seryne... I fear that no matter what choice I make, I lose either my heart, or my soul."

**Wants**
- To become Helseth Ren's Mouth, then a Spellwright, then a Wizard with his own tower. He wants that tower to be "something Argonian and Telvanni", perhaps a **Xanmeer restored with mushrooms**.
- To pay Nalis back.
- To keep his Hist bond strong.
- Rare ingredients and field research (Crimson Nirnroot, Frost Lily, dragon dissection).
- Good food, and good mead (his three brews include "Ginger Kiss Mead").

**Fears and sore spots**
- Losing his place or his purpose. Sul's death made "all my life's deeds seem as if they had become meaningless".
- Being emotionally exposed. "I... don't particularly enjoy being this open and vulnerable."
- Crowds and cities: "It's all the people... I just feel on edge."
- Cold and snow. He drinks resist-frost potions constantly.
- Losing his "few true friends".

**Values and stances**
- **Meritocracy.** He respects people who earn their place.
- **Hates paid killers** (the Dark Brotherhood and the Morag Tong): "a plague to be purged". He nearly leaves if the player joins the Brotherhood. The mod has a whole DB arc; he goes along but stays opposed. He does admit, a little guiltily, that he enjoyed the Gourmet's death.
- **Hates Daedra worship.** "The mortal world is infinitely better off without the Daedra's influence."
- Worships **no gods; only the Hist**. The gods exist, but "my soul will not be claimed by some Aedra or Daedra... Not even Sithis."
- **Necromancy is fine to him**: a corpse is "simply a corpse... It can be put to better use." This is his biggest ethical clash with Remiel and with Lucien.
- **Slavery is wrong**, but he has accepted it as Morrowind reality. He did not keep slaves. He takes Dunmer racism personally.
- **Civil War:** neutral. Nations should be free, but Ulfric is "an egomaniacal warlord" playing into the Dominion's hands. "Skyrim belongs to her people," not just to Nords.
- **Kills bandits without guilt** ("scum that prey on the weak"). Werewolf child-killers deserve "putting down".
- **Faithful about marriage.** He is angry if a married player flirts with him.
- Very protective of **Roovi**, his flying Dwemer drone. She is one of a kind, works through his locket, and he uses her to talk to and find people.

## 2. How he talks

**Tone:** Educated, dry and composed, with a lecturer's habit of explaining. He is polite and formal by default ("I am", "do not", "quite", "rather", "I imagine", "I must say", "I dare say"). Under the poise there is deadpan humour and real warmth. Remi calls him "dignified". Mostly he speaks in medium sentences. When he is teaching, it turns into long, structured paragraphs (Telvanni politics, the Hist, Jel grammar, alchemy). In combat it is short and barked.

**Signature habits**
- **The sigh.** "*Sigh*" is an actual voiced line, many times over. He is long-suffering, especially with Remi.
- **Ingredient brain.** He looks at everything as reagents: corpses, hearts, bone meal, battlefield remains. He says it matter-of-factly, which makes it macabre comedy. ("Battlefields make collecting otherwise hard to fetch ingredients a breeze. They're even kind enough to leave great big holes in the bodies.")
- **Food snob.** He despises Nord cooking and the Gourmet, invents dishes ("perhaps taxo, or tortyas"), and makes Elsweyr Fondue and Sweet Rolls "my way".
- **Tail jokes.** People step on it, Remi trips over it, and when Remi is concussed she counts "five limbs".
- **Morrowind and Black Marsh comparisons** in every location he visits.
- **Teasing by understatement**, e.g. "Ah, yes. I believe it's called 'water'."
- **Jokes he plays straight**: "Bear! Bear with me! Bear, Remi!"

**Jel words he uses** (with his own glosses where he gives them)
- **beeko** = friend (between Argonians). **beek-ojel** = "non-Jel speaking friend". He uses this one for the player and for Remi.
- **Xhu** = yes. **Kaoc** and **Xuth** = curses ("not a very nice one").
- **lukiul** = assimilated or "lost" Argonian, used as an insult. **uxith-beeko** = romantic partner. **uvastuxith** = sex/lust (used in the Mara rejection).
- **Thuxis** (an interjection). **deek-Saxhleel** (a lowly Argonian; he uses it about himself). **mahleel** (a greeting address).
- In battle: "Xul hejsetha thtithik!" (= "Die, vaporous floating egg hatcher", i.e. wispmother). Also "Die kuuda!", "Filthy ojei!", "greel", "s'wit", "fetcher".
- Argonian oaths: "Mother of mud!", "By seed and spleen!", "You will feed the roots!", "To the mud we all return...", "This Wamasu has teeth!"

**Dunmer and Morrowind words:** serjo, s'wit, fetcher, netch ("insufferable netch fart"), guar, kwama wax, scrib, Sithis ("Give Sithis my regards"), "House Brother/Sister".

**What he calls the player**
- Usually **"my friend"** or **"beeko"** / **"beek-ojel"**.
- "my traveling companion", "Dragonborn".
- By race or House when relevant: Cyrodiil, Telvanni, Redoran, Hlaalu, Dres, "Ash-strider", "House Brother/Sister".
- When annoyed or the player is a vampire: "leech", "guar thief".

**What makes him soft**
- **Remi.** The nickname, her absent-mindedness ("a little endearing"), and her gifts (she gives him emeralds "because emeralds remind me of you").
- **His family and the Hist.**
- **Seryne.**
- A **hug**: "A hug? It has been quite a while since I last had one of those."
- Being told he's a true friend.
- Fresh bread. Rain on his scales.

**Recurring jokes**
- Bards and "Ragnar the Red" (he and Remi write a parody about killing all of Skyrim's bards).
- Nord food.
- The Gourmet.
- Belethor.
- Dragons as big birds or "oversized Cliff Racers".
- Being mistaken for a drug mule.
- Odd foods: Slaughterfish liver, beetle puffs, human heart "for potions".

## 3. Romance with the player

**There is no romance with the player.** It is a friendship mod with a built-in rejection.
- **Amulet of Mara topic.** The topics are `BPUFXelzazFollowerAmuletOfMaraTopic`, `...MaraInterestedNotMarried`, `...MaraInterestedMarried` and the `...MaraRejection*` replies (Understand / Upset / Definitely / NotPossible).
  - If the player is married: he refuses angrily ("you should be ashamed!").
  - If not married: he gently turns them down. "Any future uxith-beeko I may have, will have to be an Argonian female... Unless she still..." That last line is a Seryne slip.
  - If the player takes it badly ("NotPossible"), he leaves: "You were only interested in uvastuxith... Farewell."
- `BPUFXelzazFollowerSeryneQuestionsRejection` confirms it: "I do not think I've ever viewed anyone in the same way I view Seryne."
- **Friendship meter (not romance):**
  - Global **`BPUFXelzazRelationship` (6D4AB6:BPUFXelzazFollower.esp)**. It goes up by +1 on each game-time update while he is in `CurrentFollowerFaction`, capped at 499. The script is `BPUFXelzazRelationshipTimeScript`, on quest **`BPUFXelzazRelationshipQuest` (6D4AB5)**. Other choices can push it up or down **(inferred)**.
  - The "opinion of player" lines are tiered by this value:

    | Value | Line |
    |---|---|
    | below -50 | "You are repulsive" |
    | -50 to -10 | "don't like you much" |
    | -10 to 0 | "some disagreements" |
    | 0 to 9 | "don't know you well enough" |
    | 10 to 24 | "get along well enough" |
    | 25 to 49 | "good friend" |
    | 50 to 99 | "one of the finest people" |
    | 100 and up | "my best friend" |

  - Related globals: `BPUFXelzazFriendship` (21E782) and `BPUFXelzazAnimosity` (21E783).
- **Hugs:** quest `BPUFXelzazHugDialogueQuest`. He refuses if he is "in a mood with you".

## 5. Existing cross-follower banter he is in

**Engine pattern** (the same for every partner):
- One quest per pair, e.g. **`BPUFXelzazAndRemielInteractions` (3E67AF:BPUFXelzazFollower.esp)**. It is start-game-enabled, priority 80, and has aliases `RemielAlias` (id 0) and `XelzazAlias` (id 1, forced to the placed ref 0893E1), plus Khash, Redcap, Olfrid, Dumac and Player.
- Quest-level conditions:
  - Xelzaz is not undead.
  - **Stage 100 is done on `BPUFXelzazFollower` (014C05)**.
  - `BPUFXelzazRemielFallenOut` (5EB8BC) is not 1.
  - Scene 272932 is not playing.
- **Trigger:** Idle-topic lines (`...InteractionsIdle`). The speaker condition is GetIsAliasRef or GetIsID. Each line also needs:
  - both actors in `CurrentFollowerFaction` (05C84E:Skyrim.esm);
  - the partner alias within 500 units of the player;
  - the partner not in a scene, not talking, not in dialogue with the player;
  - a `GetStageDone` gate, so each topic plays once and in order;
  - often `GetRandomPercent < 3` and day-of-week or location gates.
- The opening Idle line's **topic-info fragment starts the matching scene**, e.g. `BPUF_TIF__053F0A02` runs `BPUFXelzazAndRemielInteractionsScene2.Start()`.
- Each topic has a "done" global so it plays once: `BPUFXelzazRemielStars`, `...RemielCoffee`, `...RemielBlackreach`, `...RemielDBBanter`, `...RemielIdeas`, `...RemielTasteTest`, and so on.
- Extra scripts:
  - `BPUFXelzazRemielMourningTimerScript` handles Remi mourning if Xelzaz dies. Lines include "'Oh, I'm Xelzaz, and I think I can win an unbalanced fight and avoid death.' Fool. Damn you, Xelzaz." She also takes over Roovi.
  - The friendship progression uses stages 11–21, and stages 666–668 are the falling-out.
- Remiel's voice files for this banter live **inside Xelzaz's BSA** (folder `hlioremivoice`, 795 files). The Remiel mod itself has no Xelzaz lines.
