# KAIDAN - character sheet (for the follower-banter mod)

Built from 12,147 of his own voiced lines. A line counts as his only when a KaiVoice audio file exists for it, so lines spoken by other characters are left out. They come from 0Kaidan.esp, Kaidan - Immersive Features.esp (IF), BWB - Kaidan 2 Extra Dialogue.esp, Kaidan and Inigo Meet.esp, 018AuriKaidanBanter.esp and Lucien.esp. The KR2_Deadlands quest reuses 0Kaidan.esp quests. Deadlands ships no KaiVoice files of its own. The full grouped dump is in `..\byq\<QuestEditorID>.txt` next to this file.

## 1. Who he is

**Race.** His record says Redguard (RedguardRace). In the story he is **Akaviri**, "possibly the last living Akaviri". He never knew his mother's race until the Akaviri cache quest ("I don't know the race of my mother").

**How he was raised**
- He was raised by **Brynjar**, a Nord bounty hunter who fought in the Great War. Brynjar "lost his faith in the Empire after the Concordat".
- It was a nomadic childhood spent crossing Tamriel and Skyrim. "I learned to use a sword before I'd even learned how to read."
- He spent a year of his childhood in the Reach mountains. He spent a lot of his youth in Elsweyr (with Inigo), and he says Skyrim "feels more like home than High Rock or Cyrodiil ever did".
- When he turned 13, Brynjar left him alone in the wilderness with flint, a knife and an empty waterskin. It took him two days to get back.
- Brynjar's sayings are a running motif:
  - "If you're not a little afraid, you're not understanding the situation."
  - "Wherever you go, there you are."
  - "Chip away at it" (Brynjar meant chopping wood).

**Brynjar's death.** Brynjar drank to fight his war demons. One winter night he went out for something stronger and was found frozen on the temple steps. The priests called it "exposure". Kaidan felt anger rather than grief: "he just left."

**His mother and the sword**
- His mother's sword is his only clue to his bloodline. The Thalmor wanted to know about it.
- His mother was linked to the **Blades / an Akaviri clan** that the Thalmor wiped out ("a recount of genocide").
- **Tarben** was Brynjar's Blades "brother-in-arms" who never came back from "a mission".
- The Akaviri cache quest (Northwind mountain, then Viinturuth's tomb and the dragon burial mounds) ends with a letter from his mother and a prophecy about the Last Dragonborn. He then calls himself the player's **"Dragonguard"**.
- He believes his mother was **murdered** because of his birth ("if it hadn't been for birthing me...").
- His father is unknown: "I wonder if I'll ever get to learn more about who my father was."

**The dark years: the Blooded Dawn**
- After Brynjar died he took on Brynjar's worst habits: drink and **moon-sugar** (and skooma, which he mentions to Inigo).
- He joined the **Blooded Dawn**, a **Mehrunes Dagon** cult. They welcomed him after he killed their leader for a bounty.
- He has a **face tattoo**, inked with blood on an altar. He calls it "a curse".
- **The farm near Leyawiin:** the cult burned a stable while a child hid in the hay. "I never participated... but I stood by."
- He turned on the cult. His lover **Rosalind**, the cult's conjurer priestess, tried to burn him alive and summoned Dremora.
- **Deadlands questline ("Blood and Fire" / KMD):** Dagon's cult comes back for revenge. Rosalind survived. She is killed in the end. In KR2 Deadlands, Mehrunes Dagon himself speaks (KR2KMDDeathQuest). I am **unsure** whether Kaidan can die in that branch.

**How the player meets him.** He was trying to cross into Cyrodiil at Pale Pass and collecting bounties around Falkreath. The Thalmor took him at his lake camp and tortured him in the Abandoned Prison ("they would whip me until I almost lost consciousness"). The player frees him, and he swears a **life debt** (quest K01 "A Bounty for the Hunter").

**Other people from his past**
- **Adalyn ("Addy")**: a noble girl he tried to run away with as a teen. Brynjar caught them. She was married off to a courtier and later wrote saying "she'd wait for me". She is not a romance rival, and he settles it in the romance path.
- **Erik** (Rorikstead): a childhood friend.
- **The Companions:** Farkas is his "best mate". Vilkas is his sparring partner. Aela teases him. There is a Companions questline, KaiComp "Vengeance and Honor": he is given his sword at Jorrvaskr, Kodlak is mentioned, and the player becomes "my Harbinger and my love".

**Quest list (EditorIDs):** K01 rescue · KINV "All of this Past" (Akaviri relics) · KMD / KMDQuest "Blood and Fire" + KMDNightmares "Ashes of His Past" + KMDRosalindScenePost "Absolution by Dragon Fire" · KaiComp · KDBro (he can follow you into the Dark Brotherhood) · KaiJail "(Another) Bounty for the Hunter" (he gets arrested in Riften) · KaiSandwich · KaiHoneymoon "With You All The Way" · weddings HFSmallWedding / HFBigWeddingIF / HFWRWedding.

**What he wants**
- To belong somewhere: "It's wondering what staying put for once might look like. Belonging somewhere."
- To learn his heritage.
- For his life "to amount to more than surviving the next fight".
- Later on: a home, the player, and kids (lines at K04 85+).

**What he fears**
- Losing the player: "I dreamt that you'd left me... I guess it must be my greatest fear."
- His own capacity for violence: "What makes you think I won't hurt you someday?"
- That he is "the villain of this story".
- Being caged again.

**What he values**
- Loyalty and oaths.
- Plain common sense over schooling.
- Protecting the weak. He feels guilt for having "stood by".
- His rules: "finish what you start; don't rely on anyone who hasn't earned it; don't ignore your instincts."

**Faith.** He leans toward **Kyne** ("patron of travellers"). He swears by the Nine, Mara and Ysmir. He distrusts faith that turns into "destiny". He hates Daedra, and Dagon most of all.

**Small facts**
- Favourite colour: blue. Favourite season: autumn. Favourite time of day: just before sunrise.
- Favourite food: horker sandwiches. He also loves sweet rolls, steak and ale. He likes Nord mead because Brynjar drank it. He hates Argonian Bloodwine and charred skeever.
- He wanted a dog as a boy. He has a dog, **Meeko**, through the Meeko patch.
- Weapons: a longsword or a two-hander, a war bow, heavy armour. He "can't sneak for shit".
- He doesn't trust magic but admires the player's.
- He thinks his name means "ghost story" (Kai + Dan).
- **Politics:** he is anti-Thalmor and anti-Empire ("died two hundred years ago with Martin Septim"). He is also anti-Ulfric ("a racist with an ego").

## 2. How he talks

**Accent and dialect.** He speaks with a northern English / Geordie flavour:
- "Haway, pet", "me" for "my" ("me arse", "me mind", "me-self").
- "Aye", "Oi", "mate", "lass/lad", "bloody", "sod off", "gobshite", "wanker", "pissed" meaning drunk, "fooking".

**Sentence shape**
- Usually short to medium. He uses a lot of trailing "eh?", "y'know", "heh" and "hehe".
- His speeches are emotional and run long. They often start with "Right, here it goes..." and include self-interruptions ("I mean, uh...").

**Swearing.** He swears heavily and casually, mostly "fuck me", "bloody hell" and "shit". He swears less in tender scenes, and almost not at all with kids or nobles.

**Oaths.** "Ysmir's beard", "Dagon's danglies", "Stendarr's mercy on me", "By the Nine", "Mara only knows".

**Pet names for the player**
- Most often: **love**, **little dragon**, **my dragon**, darling or darlin', sweetheart.
- Also: lass or lad, trouble, kitten, pet, Dragonborn, "that's my girl/man", "my unshakeable dragon".
- On the "I love you" menu: my deadly nightshade, wild wolf, my fearless warrior, my sweet siren.

**Recurring jokes and bits**
- Horker everything, including a Forrest-Gump-style list of horker dishes.
- Terrible puns ("axe-ident", "wooden tit", cheddar), followed by "Did that joke fall flat, little dragon?"
- "Dangly bits", including freezing them.
- Drunk stories: a Bruma monastery with no eyebrows, Khajiit "warm sands", being frozen to a cart.
- Farkas pranks (spiders, chicken feathers in his boots, card games where Farkas "can't bluff").
- Limericks (KLimericks).
- Self-scolding while distracted by the player: "Focus, Kaidan.", "Bad Kaidan.", "Rawr."

**What he teases the player about**
- Showing off ("You bloody showoff!") and getting into trouble ("trouble with a capital T").
- Eating dragon souls.
- Alchemy ("eating weird shit").
- Thieving ("maybe we skip visiting Riften").
- Snoring.
- Their song "The Dragonborn Comes" ("I have questions...").

**What makes him soft**
- The player's smile, being healed, being told he is good.
- Talk of his mother being proud.
- Children.
- The player being sick or sad. Then he becomes very caring: "It's unfair that we laugh together but you cry alone."

**When pushed about his past.** He gets defensive, then angry, then breaks and apologises. The KMDNightmares scene is the clearest example.

## 4. His opinions of other followers

None of his lines mention **Gore, Val Serano, Remiel or Xelzaz**. A new mod would be first ground there.

- **Inigo** (Kaidan and Inigo Meet.esp):
  - Warm and brotherly ("Listen brother", "I'm glad we're friends, you and I").
  - Archery rivalry ("Still think you're a better archer than me?").
  - Teases him about skooma and sweetrolls.
  - Jokes he'll "shave your tail when you're sleeping".
  - They bond over Elsweyr and both having been prisoners.
- **Auri** (018AuriKaidanBanter.esp):
  - Mildly exasperated and dry ("Do you have any hobbies other than being nosy and obnoxious?", "I do not brood.").
  - Cautious: "She seems nice enough. Are you certain we can trust her? ... I'll keep an eye on her, just in case."
- **Lucien** (Lucien.esp has Kaidan-voiced lines):
  - Prickly. He is touchy about being called stupid ("Of course I can read. I'm not a moron.").
  - Threatening: "Maybe this barbarian will be stupid enough to forget to guard your scrawny neck".
  - Puzzled: "Every day I understand you less and less."
  - Thaws a little ("Good morning, Lucien. Sleep well?").
- **Serana:** protective, unafraid ("I'm not afraid of you, or your curse"), asks after her family.
- **Lydia:** grudging respect ("Calm your tits Lydia... you're not too bad with that sword"). The quest is nicknamed "Still a potato but mildly funnier".
- **Farkas:** best mate and constant prank target. **Vilkas:** "grumpy git", sparring partner. **Aela:** respects her and thanks her at the wedding.
- **Others:** Mjoll ("Mjoll is badass"), Erik (childhood friend), Brelyna (archery contest with the twins).
- **Cicero:** finds him irritating but funny.
- **Barbas:** "I've grown fond of Barbas."

## 5. Existing cross-follower banter (so the new mod copies the method and avoids repeats)

**A. Kaidan and Inigo Meet.esp, quest `KaiandInigo` (000842, StartGameEnabled, priority 60)**
- Aliases Kaidan and Inigo are filled by UniqueActor.
- **Trigger:** an Idle topic `KaiandInigoIdles` (000800). Kaidan says INFO 000802 when all of these hold:
  - GetIsID Kaidan
  - quest stage ≥ 10
  - GetInWorldspace Tamriel
  - GetRandomPercent > 1
  - player not sneaking
  - **GetDistance to Inigo ref ≤ 600**
  - not IsInScene
- Its fragment (`PostIntroScript`) calls `InigoRotate.RandomConvo()`.
- That function steps a counter global `InigoRoll` through 1→20 and starts one Scene per step.
- Some scenes are skipped unless a condition holds:
  - exterior only (not LocTypeCity, LocTypeSettlement or an interior)
  - LocTypeInn
  - LocTypePlayerHouse
  - LocSetDraugrCrypt
  - K04 ≥ 70, ≥ 75 or ≥ 100
- The intro (INFO 000801) needs: stage 0, exterior, distance ≤ 500, not in the prison cell.
- **Quest-level DialogConditions** block it during:
  - IF's STFU globals (STFUVarNPCGeneral, KNPCStart, KaiWeddingNukeIdles, denialisntjustariver, AubrieandVaelHateYou)
  - KaiJailedVar
  - the Dagon shrine and Deadland cells
  - AnimationFaction on the player
  - MQ104, C02, C04–C06, the Alduin and C01 scenes
  - KaidanDoNothingPkg
  - High Hrothgar and the Abandoned Prison
- **Topics already used:** Introduction, PostIntro, Archery1/2/3, Sweetroll, Tavern, Tavern2, Spiders, Menace, Cursing, Exes, Relationship, 12, 13, Random, Travelled (Elsweyr), PlayerHome, Crypt, Night (starry skies), Chicken (evil farm animals), Wedding, MetMatch ("shot through the heart").
