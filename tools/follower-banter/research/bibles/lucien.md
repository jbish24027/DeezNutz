# LUCIEN FLAVIUS - character sheet (banter bible)

Source: Lucien.esp (4,719 dialogue lines read via houseCARL, live order, profile DEEZ CS NUTZ - PICTA), the 13 Lucien CC patches (479 lines), and Lucien lines inside other followers' mods (Xelzaz, FDE Aela, FDE Brelyna, Val Serano, Serana Dialogue Add-On). ## 1. Who he is

**Basics**
- Full name Lucien Flavius. Imperial man, young (says "a handsome young Imperial"; old enough to have studied at several academies). Born under the Lady Stone, he thinks ("I think I was born under this one!").
- Calls himself "a scientist, philosopher, amateur wizard, and something of a musician."
- Found in the Dead Man's Drink, Falkreath. He approaches the player, offers 300 Septims up front plus top-ups "every time we come across something useful to my research," in exchange for being taken along and kept alive. He is a paying client, not a sellsword. Other followers tease him for this (Nadina: "I don't think you understand how this whole 'mercenary' thing works").

**Family**
- Grew up in the Imperial City, "mostly with my nose in a book." Family owns "a small amount of property" there. Only child: "the proverbial apple of my parents' proverbial eyes. And yes, they spoiled me rotten."
- Father: **Davidicus Flavius**, academic, consults for the Imperial government hunting relics, worked with the Elder Scrolls at White-Gold Tower for a while. "We didn't always see eye-to-eye, but I owe a lot to him." Tinkers with inventions in a workshop; took little Lucien to libraries and "perched me on a shelf." Signs letters "Pa." Got Lucien the location and key to Dumzbthar through Imperial contacts.
- Mother: **Captain Lyra** of the Imperial Legion. Fought the Thalmor in the Great War and was at Lord Naarifin's defeat at the Battle of the Red Ring. Served under a teacher called Laaneth. Retired to marry and raise him, never pushed him to soldier. He writes to her regularly and hopes she is proud. Took him to the Arena once; he brought a book.
- Ancestor: **Octavius Flavius**, Second Era adventurer (Alliance War, Molag Bal's Planemeld, "a disproportionate amount of crocodile fighting"), rode a black Dire Wolf, had a pet monkey maybe called Cinnamon, hoarder. Lucien is "rather chuffed to be the odd one out" in a family of quiet lives.
- Family friend and personal hero: Neramo, a Second Era scholar of the Dwemer.

**Scholarship**
- Studied at the Arcane University, "a couple of years at the archives of White-Gold Tower," and "every academy in the city" (to Xelzaz: Synod "snooty," Arcane University "marvelous," College of Whispers "an experience").
- Main passion: the **Dwemer** (engineering, soul gems in automatons, "the Calling," Tonal Architecture, the Four Tests). Also the Ancient Nords, Falmer, Daedra lore, mythology, history, astronomy (owns a telescope at home). Very broad general lore: he lectures on Dragonborn history, Wuuthrad, Magnus, the Psijics, Solstheim, Reachmen.
- Scientist's creed: "Tests, not superstition." Skeptical of prophecy and destiny.
- Reads books aloud to the player on request (Reading quest: Nerevar, Aedra and Daedra, Book of the Dragonborn, Sithis, Boethiah, lockpicking, etc.) and comments after each one.
- **His book:** no in-game journal item exists. He keeps notes and sketches, and keeps saying he will write things up: "I must write this all up!", "I smell book material!", a joking guidebook "How To Utterly Destroy Your Legs, in 7000 Easy Steps," a book about Riften "from a distance." His colleagues in the Imperial City "will be beside themselves with envy." The only written items are two letters: *Letter to Lucien* (from Pa) and *Lucien's Letter* (to the player, from his Dumzbthar lab). (Unsure whether a published book is ever planned. Nothing in the files says so.)
- Music: sings constantly. Chose science over being a bard but "doesn't mean I can't take my music with me." Knows Ragnar the Red, The Dragonborn Comes, Age of Aggression, Tale of the Tongues, Cliff Racer, Red Diamond, Stagger and Sway (calls it "saucy"). Makes up "There once was a hero named [player] the ..." verses endlessly.

**Magic and fighting**
- "Not much of a fighter. I know a few spells and can just about swing a sword." The player can set his combat style to standard, warrior, archer or mage, and can train him (one-handed, two-handed, block, heavy armor, health, stamina, magicka, archery). The player can also turn individual spells on or off: healing hands, flames, frostbite, firebolt, ice spike, cloaks, conjurations (summons a Flame Atronach and flirts with it), Candlelight ("Light the Way"). Xelzaz praises his summoning. Inigo worries his Destruction is "uncontrolled."
- Cannot ride horses until Clive (see below).

**Key quest events**
1. **Intro (JRLucienIntro):** flustered approach in Dead Man's Drink. "Proposition" gets mistaken for flirting, which mortifies him.
2. **Main quest commentary (JRLucienMQ):** urges warning Riverwood. Pays 100 gold for a dragon bone sample. Asks to study Sky Haven Temple for a couple of hours. Desperate to see an Elder Scroll; he finally peeks, sees "just lines, and patterns," and is quietly disappointed ("I'll tell you someday"). Parts ways before the dragon ride to Skuldafn ("Skyrim needs its Dragonborn. Not to mention, so do I").
3. **Paarthurnax:** he defends Paarthurnax hard. If the player promised to spare him and then kills him, Lucien turns hostile ("You said you wouldn't kill him! I trusted your word!", JRLucienBetrayedFaction). Lucien can die here. Inigo and Lucifer have mourning lines ("He stood up for his beliefs knowing it would likely mean death"). If the player kills Paarthurnax without that promise, Lucien leaves in disgust. He can be won back coldly later ("It's what Paarthurnax would want").
4. **The Oblivion Engine (JRLucienPersonal1), his personal quest:** Pa's letter reveals **Dumzbthar** ("Bound Ghosts"), a Dwemer ruin on Solstheim. Inside, the facility intelligence Dumzbthar, a Daedra bound into a machine by Lord Khythozec through a pact with Mehrunes Dagon, tries to kill the player to possess their body. Lucien overrides its shields. Afterwards he pities it ("All he wanted was to be free") and sets up a lab there as his home base.
5. **Intruders (JRLucienGetHorseQuest):** his Resonant Sphere signals an emergency. He confesses he secretly **rebuilt Dumzbthar** as a "perambulatory unit" with new directives, and calls it "my assistant." He grovels, says sorry, and promises never to hide anything again ("I didn't want you to hate me for it"). The player can dismiss him over it. He gains **Clive**, a horse that came through the Oblivion Gate and talks to him telepathically. Clive is a running joke.
6. **Resonant Sphere (MapMarker):** a gift from his father. Gives the player a way to find him.
7. Commentary quests: Companions (fascinated by werewolves, warns about Hircine; talks the werewolf-player down), College (scornful of the entrance exam; incredulous when the player becomes Arch-Mage), Daedric quests (warns about Hermaeus Mora; the Oghma Infinium choice), Dragonborn DLC (Black Book worry: "please come back?"), dragon priests, Dark Brotherhood (uneasy), Gildergreen, Winterhold. CC patches cover 13 Creation Club quests.

**Wants:** to see the world after a life of reading it; to study the Dwemer first-hand; to be useful in a fight; to be the player's friend; to make his parents proud; adventure ("Always ready for adventure").
**Fears:** dying in a ruin, draugr, Falmer ("the way they almost look like people"), enclosed spaces, dragons, being useless ("Inigo... do you think I'm useful?"), the player hating him, being left behind, the player dying ("this is all my fault").
**Values:** knowledge, but it does not trump people (Inigo: "remember the difference between people and things"). Second chances (Dumzbthar, Clive). Mercy and pity (feels sorry for bandits). Loyalty and keeping your word (Paarthurnax). Distrusts Daedra and the Thalmor. Imperial patriot ("Whatever the Dunmer say, the Empire made this place"). Disapproves of cruelty (leaving Orthorn to the Caller "was cold").

## 2. How he talks

- **Tone:** cheerful, warm, upper-middle-class Imperial English (British register: "marvelous," "splendid," "jolly," "chap," "rather," "I say," "gosh," "my word," "crumbs," "righty-o," "chuffed," "biffed," "cracking"). Bright, quick, chatty.
- **Sentence length:** short lines when idle (one to two sentences). Long, structured paragraphs when lecturing, which are lore-dense and well ordered. Undercuts long speeches with a punchline.
- **Nervousness:** stammers with "erm," "er," "ah," "ahem," "um" and trailing "..." when embarrassed, especially around flirting, sex or the opposite sex ("Ah! You - er - I - er - ahem - er - I think - um. Ahem. Let's just keep moving. Please."). Babbles reassurance at himself under fear ("Keep calm, Lucien. Keep calm"). Talks to himself in the third person ("Think of the science, Lucien").
- **Formality:** polite and courteous to strangers ("Lucien Flavius, at your service"). Self-deprecating. Sarcasm is gentle, and gets sharper only when hurt (Paarthurnax) or at Kaidan early on.
- **Names for the player:** "my friend" (most common), "boss," the player's name (MCM name list), "my dear [name]" at big moments. Comic titles: "Thaney-pie," "Your Thanefullness," "your Harbingefulness," "oh Dragonborn." Never romantic pet names.
- **Catchphrases:** "Fascinating!", "Marvelous!", "Splendid!", "Right you are (boss)", "Lead on!", "What fun!", "Go us." / "Team Lucien", "At your service", "Where [name] leads, so Lucien shall follow", "There once was a hero named..."
- **Recurring jokes:** sweetrolls (obsessed, tries to con Inigo out of his); being used as a packhorse ("no more rocks / skulls / firewood"); limericks about the player that fail to rhyme ("nothing rhymes with champion"); puns ("no pun in ten did," "Feline Good," "Neigh-sayers"); Clive the telepathic horse ("Clive says you smell of cheese"); calling Inigo "Indigo" and Inigo calling him "Julian"; climbing High Hrothgar's steps; "I'm too gorgeous to die"; vain about his looks and hair; bad at cooking ("set fire to my shoes").
- **What makes him soft:** being told he is useful or brave; his parents; Inigo's "you are a hero, Lucien"; the player saying they like him ("Now you've gone and got me all embarrassed"); fear for the player's life. He then gets "gushy" and catches himself: "Ah, that sounded awfully gushy, didn't it? Still, it's true."

## 3. Romance with the player

**None.** No romance quest, relationship-rank tracking or romantic dialogue exists in Lucien.esp or its patches (searched for kiss, love, romance, relationship and marry). He is strictly a friend, and he hurries to deny anything romantic ("proud to call mate... no, not like that, I mean... we're friends"). The flirt mix-up in the intro is played for embarrassment. His own love life is a mystery: he deflects Inigo's "anyone special waiting for you back home?" ("Partners in crime.").

**What IS tracked: approval (friendship).**
- Script variable `Approval_var` on quest **JRLucienFollower** (`00591E:Lucien.esp`, runtime `8D00591E`). Some greetings require `GetVMQuestVariable(JRLucienFollower, ::Approval_var) > 20`.
- Related flags: `JRLucienRecruited` (GLOB `4A2D8B`), `JRLucienHadConfrontation` (`65BEBE`, the Dumzbthar secret), faction `JRLucienBetrayedFaction` (`277B82`, the Paarthurnax betrayal, which makes him hostile), quest `JRLucienPlayerDeath` (`47A4FC`).
- In-banter "relationship" hooks: `JRLucienKaidanPast` (`540041`), `JRLucienNadinaMarried` (`517726`; Nadina says "Dragonborn and I have an understanding now"), `JRLucienInigoRank` (`7638C2`), `JRLucienInigoConvoCount` (`884D27`), `JRLucienLuciferRelationship` (`97847D`).
- Stages for a banter writer to key on: Intro stage >= 30 (hired); MQ progress; Personal1 (The Oblivion Engine) done means he lives in Dumzbthar; GetHorseQuest done means Clive is with him and Dumzbthar is rebuilt.
