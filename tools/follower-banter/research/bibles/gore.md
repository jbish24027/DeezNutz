Source: about 3,550 of his own dialogue lines (GORE.esp, Gore - Vigilant.esp, Gore - SaSEC.esp) plus all 253 lines of Remiel's HLIOGoreBanter quest (HLIORemi.esp), read from the live load order on 2026-10-18. Mod folder: `C:\1. AUTHORIA\mods\Gore`.

## 1. Who he is

**Race and look.** He is a Nord. He uses his own race record, HDGoreRace (40217B:GORE.esp), and his father was Imperial. He is lanky and can't put on weight ("I've always been lanky"). He can only grow a chin beard and moustache. He has acne that he hides under war paint, eyes that are "more common down in Cyrodiil", and a chipped tooth from a horse kick while shoeing a sick soldier's horse. He has some burn scars ("I lost some of my good looks to that stuff"). He wears his mother's amulet. He fights with a greatsword and has bare arms "for mobility". He guesses his age at "19, maybe 20".

**Name.** "Gore" was a camp nickname from the Orcs: "closer to an insult, but I guess it grew on me". Pate tells him his real name is **Mattias**. Gore hates it: "the most pretentious Imperial-sounding thing I have heard in my life". He goes by Gore.

**Origin.**
- His mother, **Heide**, grew up in Helgen and worshipped Kyne. She was killed in a Thalmor raid on a Talos-worshipping farm village near Bruma when he was too young to remember.
- The **Band of the Raven** found him "stunned and alone among the corpses" and raised him. This was a mercenary band in Cyrodiil: "a ragtag band of borderline bandits... blades for hire". His many surrogate "Fathers" were mostly Orcs. Orcs taught him the sword.
- The leader, **Jo-Lee**, taught him tactics and logistics ("A good plan... is simple"). Jo-Lee was a "cruel mentor" who "wore a mask, a damned good one".
- Gore made cavalry lieutenant. The men mocked him over his horse **Gorse** ("they found a way to make me feel humiliated even on the day I was pinned"), and he still misses that horse.
- He had no schooling. He learned the world by listening to scholar-mercenaries argue at night. He could not read. Remiel teaches him, and in the Vigilant arc he says "I learned to read, blood. It took me months".

**Trauma.**
- As a boy of "7 or 8" he was abused by criminals Jo-Lee hired cheaply out of prisons. "That's how I was blooded": his first kill was one of those men.
- "Blooded" means first kill and "twice-blooded" means hardened to killing. He calls himself "thrice blooded".
- He killed conscripts and "made orphans... I killed them", just to get back to his bunk and a hot meal. He carries real guilt for this.
- He has never had sex or held hands: "I haven't had sex for a reason."

**Key quest events, in order:**
1. **Raven's Flight** (HD1GoreMeet). He is caught in a bear trap near Helgen and hunted by the Thalmor.
2. **Wanted** (HD1GoreEscort). The player kills his Thalmor pursuers and he swears himself to the player.
3. **The Drunken Huntsman** (HD1GoreDH).
4. **Blood Ties** (HD1GoreQuest2). He tells the player about his past, then traces Pate to Dawnstar.
   - Pate was the Raven who led the survivors across the border and tried to ditch Gore.
   - Pate reveals that Jo-Lee is Gore's father, that Jo-Lee killed Gore's mother, and that Gore's name is Mattias. Then Gore kills Pate in front of Pate's wife and children.
   - The player can talk him out of killing the wife and children. Otherwise "The Apple from the Tree" (HD1GoreMurderer) has him go further and leave.
5. **To Free a Raven** (HD1GoreJailtime). He turns himself in at Dawnstar jail and the player pays 1,000 septims bail.
   - In the inn afterwards he says Jo-Lee is his father and set the Ravens up for Thalmor ransom. If romanced, this is where he confesses he "feels a certain way" about the player.
6. **Come, the Crows** (HD1GoreComeTheCrows) is the aftermath.
7. **Where We Stand** (HD1GoreInterlude). At a waterfall near Ivarstead he explains he needs to heal before any romance.
8. **Nest Left Empty** (HD1Interlude2). He confesses to being a "willing and able murderer".
9. **A New Coat** (HD1ArmorUpgrades). Taarie makes him new armor.
10. **Count Fleet** (HD1GoreHorse1). The player buys him a horse.
11. **Fear and Loathing** (HD1GoreFearAndLoathing). He meets Molag Bal at the House of Horrors and is terrified.
12. **Gore - Vigilant.esp chapter** (HD1GoreVigilant, HD1MemoriesQ "Fading Memories").
    - The player joins the Vigilants of Stendarr. He suspects Altano and finds a Molag Bal shrine under the temple.
    - He loses the player, searches for months, learns to read, and is pulled into **Coldharbour** as a soul-shriven with his memories slipping. In those memories he is a "whipped pup" under Jo-Lee and the officers.
    - The player rescues him: "That was my Mother... I'm not afraid any more!"
    - Lamae's nightmare and a hagraven also appear.
13. **SaSEC** (HD1Madness). Sheogorath: "I vote nay".

**What he wants.**
- Answers from Jo-Lee more than revenge. Later he chooses to stop chasing him: "I'm not giving a dead man and a liar another moment of my life."
- Happiness that he chooses on purpose: "I choose happiness... I'll love when others would kill."
- A found family. He wants to see the homelands the Ravens described: Hammerfell, Valenwood, Elsweyr.

**Fears.**
- Molag Bal and Coldharbour above all: "the only time I felt truly powerless" (Molag Bal ruled one of the sources of his childhood misery).
- Being hated once people know what he did. He told the player expecting them to hate him, "so that it could happen on my terms".
- Hermaeus Mora, Namira, Daedra in general. Spiders. Tombs ("We're not supposed to be here").

**Values.**
- Loyalty, and blood as a "covenant, not an obligation or a racial trait".
- He refuses to kill on command any more ("I am not a murderer, anymore").
- He respects shunned peoples: Khajiit, Dunmer, Argonians, Redguards and Orcs.
- He admires Trinimac/Malacath ("Just because you're not perfect doesn't mean you're not worthy of love"). He dislikes Talos-pride, the Thalmor and nobles.
- He is not religious. He keeps Kyne close only through his mother.

## 2. How he talks

- **Tone.** Cocky, crude, self-mocking soldier banter on the surface. Underneath he is sincere and very tender, and it comes out in long confessional speeches with hesitations ("I... err...", "Ugh...", "you know?").
- **Sentence length.** Idles and barks are short. Heart-to-hearts run five to seven sentences.
- **Swearing.** Heavy in combat: "bastard", "bitch", "freak", even "cunt". He mixes Dunmeri insults he picked up from Boethiah-worshipping Dunmer in the Ravens ("s'wit", "n'wah"). He never swears at the player except fondly ("you wretch").
- **Pet names.**
  - **"blood"** to the player, constantly. Romanced, it becomes **"blood of my blood"** and "my chosen blood".
  - "brother"/"sister" depending on the player's sex, a Ravens habit. He asks before using it and respects a non-binary correction.
  - He can also use a name the player picks from a long name list (HD1Name*/HD1Gore* globals).
- **Catchphrases and recurring jokes:**
  - "Tusk yourself", "tuskin'", "Mara's tits", "For Mara's sake", "milk drinker" (for anyone he dislikes, including Talos and Ulfric), "Gods", "Oblivion".
  - "I'M A PERPETUAL-MOTION KILLING MACHINE!" when healed.
  - "Put 'em to rest, blood" and "Witnessed." on kills.
  - Mead, always. Being a "cheap date". He hates bards and lutes. He smells bad and sweats.
  - He is the pack mule. He loves fishing ("I'm simple"). He is the "strikingly handsome Gore".
  - Bullying mages ("my bullying manual"). Hawk is the worst meal. Gorse.
  - The "two-for-one" mercenary deal and Remiel paying him 200 septims (with Remi).
- **What he teases about.**
  - The player's race (the height jokes at camp, HD1CAMPHEIGHT).
  - Nords and himself ("I'm a lot less Nord than I thought").
  - Remi's scholarship and Scrap. Bards. Jarls. Mages. Talos worshippers.
- **What makes him soft.**
  - Being thanked or called a good person. Holding hands. Campfires, auroras and sunrises.
  - His mother. Animals (Meeko, Barbas, Count Fleet). Kids.
  - Being cared for: "No one ever really cared."

**Romance arc in his words:**
- **Dawnstar inn (To Free a Raven).** He is flustered and denies it at first. Then: "I'm... a bit... different. I haven't had sex for a reason... If you feel the same way, my best advice would be to... stop."
- **Where We Stand.** He needs to work through childhood abuse first ("not the jealous type... it could be a while until I'm ready to commit").
- **Nest Left Empty.** He confesses being a killer.
- **Romance2.** He decides to stop chasing Jo-Lee for the player's sake, and they get a found-family declaration.
- **Romance3.** The oath at the shrine.

He is written as **asexual-leaning and romance-slow**. Physical affection means hand-holding, hugs and sleeping close by the fire. The camp hugs use the pa_HugA paired idle (HD1GoreController.HD1Hug()).

**Jealousy and rival reactions.** He says outright: "I'm not really the jealous type, you know?" (777AB4).
- If the player says they met someone else: "Oh, yeah. Of course... I get it. I may have gotten ahead of myself... We don't have to be anything more than blood. I still love you." (C1E82F)
- After a breakup: "I couldn't expect you to wait forever... I still stand by you, blood. I promise." (C20FCB)
- Romanced, he jokes about other lovers: "Look around, I'm sure you stepped on a few trudging across the province to get here. They're free for the taking! Unlike me. You're stuck with me now, blood." (DAAB4F)
- He has no reactions to the player romancing a specific other follower.

## 4. Opinions of other followers

Only Remiel, Auri, Taliesin and Peter (and Meeko/Barbas) have real lines. **Kaidan, Val Serano, Xelzaz, Inigo and Lucien have no written opinion lines.**
- Gore's player-alias script `HD1GoreModInteractions` (on HD1GoreFollows) declares aliases for about 65 follower mods (Kaidan 0Kaidan.esp 400F9C, Inigo.esp 008AE9, Lucien.esp 00591F, BPUFXelzazFollower.esp 0893E1 and others).
- In the plugin, only RemiFollows, RemiMeet, Remi, Auri, Peter and Taliesin are filled, and no dialogue uses the rest. None of those mods mention Gore either: I searched every INFO in the order for "Gore" and only Remi's mod refers to him.
- **This is open ground for a new mod.**

- **Remiel (Remi).** His closest follower friend.
  - Sibling energy: "Remi's cute, like a little sister or something. Don't let her get hurt."
  - He calls her "sister" as a sign of trust. She teaches him to read, and they trade insults happily ("I'd prefer it if ya' did").
  - He complains she never carries anything: "God forbid Remi has to carry anything." He respects her brains: "Couple of morons like us really needed someone like Remi."
  - Without Remi's mod, his own HD1GoreFollowerScenes quest has their trap-meeting and a reading-aloud bit: "Ugh... did Remi put you up to this?"
- **Xelzaz.** Only indirectly, through Remi. She calls Xel a great friend, and Gore answers: "Argonians are no different than you and I, and they make for great friends." He is generally pro-Argonian.
- **Auri** (HD1GoreAuriDialogue).
  - He is warm and loves Bosmer: "No one tops the company of a Bosmer". He wants her to teach him the longbow.
  - Bosmer drink and meat-diet jokes ("my mother was a bunch of old men").
  - He jokes about the Green Pact punishment, she rebukes him, and he apologises sincerely.
- **Taliesin** ("Thalmor fan-club"). Gore tells the player to help him despite his hatred of the Thalmor: "He's a person far before he's just a Thalmor." They flirt-banter ("You're simply Gore-geous").
- **Serana.** He distrusts her at first: "I don't trust this 'Serana.'" Later: "I definitely felt there was some kind of hurt there when she talked about her family."
- **Cicero.** "Can we keep him? I'll feed him and clean up after him."
- **Meeko.** He adopts him: "I got the opportunity to pay it forward, taking him in, same as you did with me."
- **Barbas.** Sad to see him go, "even if didn't let me pet him".
- **Vanilla NPCs.** "Delphine bitch... has an agenda". Mercer is untrustworthy. "Farengar is a definite 'would'". "Faendal... Bosmers make for a good time". Kodlak was owed Sovngarde.
