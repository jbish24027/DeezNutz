# REDCAP - character sheet (for OmniVoice follower banter)

Source: read straight from the live load order (profile "DEEZ CS NUTZ - PICTA"), 2026-10-18.
Read: all 2,158 dialogue lines in Redcap's plugin + his Gogh patch, all 53 lines of Remi's Redcap banter, and all 532 lines of Xelzaz's Redcap and three-way banter.
Nothing in the game was changed.

> **He is NOT a goblin.** Redcap is a **Riekling**, the little tribal creatures from Solstheim. He takes offence at being called a goblin: "Hear. Xelzaz. Say. Me. Goblin...Redcap. No. Goblin!"
> (Xelzaz calls him "goblin" and "little fetcher" on purpose.)

## 1. Who he is

**Facts from his dialogue and quests**
- **What he is.** A Riekling, which is normally a small blue creature. He grew up on **Solstheim**. He calls it **"Big Ash Rock"** and the sea **"Great Water"**. He thought the Great Water was the end of the world.
- **His tribe.** A small tribe in a village in a cave "Under Big Ash Rock", near the water. They built it from shipwreck salvage. Their village has a **pig totem**.
- **Bigpig.** His pet Bristleback boar, and his best friend.
- **Captured by the Bad Man.** A Dunmer slaver called **Manos K'han** caught him and kept him in a cage for "Twelve Moons". Manos drank, did not feed him much, and beat him with a hammer. Manos nicknamed him "Lil Red".
- **Sold to the witch.** Manos sold him across the Great Water to **Anise**, the "old woman" witch whose cabin is near Riverwood. She experimented on him:
  - Her potions turned him from blue to his current colour. Xelzaz calls him a "bright yellow Riekling".
  - Her magic made him learn to talk faster.
  - She forced a light necklace on him ("Redcap's light", the Candlelight spell).
  - She tied him to a summon spell that pulled him back whenever he ran.
  - She gave him shadow magic for stealth.
- **The red cap.** A helmet he made himself ("Redcap. Make. By. Self!"). Manos recognises him by it.
- **How you meet him.** The player finds him locked in Anise's basement, kills the witch, and he joins out of gratitude.

**His quests**
- The Cabin in the Woods.
- Back to the Rock / The Village. He returns to Solstheim and finds Bigpig, but his tribe is gone.
- You Can Be a Bigpig, Too.
- The Hands of Fate. You kill Manos and free the captured tribe. They welcome him, but he chooses to stay with the player: "You. Redcap's. Family."
- The Red God. "Red God" is **Sanguine**. Rieklings see him when they burn "Redgrass" (scathecraw) and do the "Godspeak Dance". Sanguine gets him drunk, steals his drinks, confesses his boyfriend died, and gives him the **Sanguine Rose staff**.
- Bloodmoon Rising / Wereboar Art Thou. Hircine ("Hunt God") takes Bigpig, and Bigpig becomes a **Werepig**. Redcap gets a ring and new armour. This questline includes Sinding and a murdered "youngling".
- He can become **Chief of the Thirsk Rieklings** ("Redcap. Chief! This. Hall. Mine!!!"). There is also a party quest called "King of All Rieklings".
- He comments on the main quest, the College of Winterhold, and CC Goblins. In the CC Goblins quest he meets Gogh and gets the Spear of Bitter Mercy.

**What he wants**
- Sweet rolls, above everything. A sweet roll gives him a sugar rush, which is an actual speed buff in the mod.
- Dizzydrink (any alcohol).
- Redgrass to burn.
- Shiny things.
- To keep Bigpig and his friends safe.
- To be treated as smart.

**What he fears**
- Witches and magic used on him.
- Cages.
- Vampires biting him.
- Deep dark places.
- Giants stepping on him.
- Bees.
- Not being accepted because he is "too different" now.

**What he values**
- Loyalty: "Redcap follow. Redcap help friend. Always."
- Sharing within the tribe ("In. Same tribe. We. Share."). He does not share with outsiders.
- Revenge on witches. He hates hagravens ("Bird-witch") and Forsworn witches.
- Animals. Every dog is a "pup". He loves Meeko and Vigilance.

**My guesses (marked)**
- He seems to be an adult who acts childlike. His dialogue never gives his age.
- He is male: Remi, Xelzaz and Inigo all say "he" and "him". His voice actor is female (see section 7).

## 2. How he talks

### Grammar rules
1. **One word per sentence**, each followed by a full stop. Example: "Redcap. Make. By. Self!"
   - A few set phrases stay joined: "We go", "I wait", "Big Ash Rock", "Sweet roll", "Thank you".
2. **No articles. No verb endings. No "is" or "are".** Example: "Bigpig. Picky."
3. **Pronouns**
   - "Me" means I: "Me. Like. Cold!"
   - He talks about himself as **"Redcap"** in the third person.
   - "Him" and "Her" are subjects: "Him. Mad...A lot."
   - "Them" means they: "Them. Suck."
4. **"..." pauses** show hesitation or sadness: "Redcap...Was. Blue. Most. Of. Life. Then...Witch."
5. **Long names get split into syllables.** Examples: "Vigi. Lance.", "Mee-ko", "Tel. Vah. Ni?", "Ivar. Stead", "Riff. Ten. Riff. Nine...", "Fall. Buh. Thartz", "Bran. Dee."
6. **Sound tags** in asterisks: *grunt*, *excited Riekling noises*, *NOM*, *burp*, *stutters*, *shudders*.
7. **Shouting** goes in CAPITALS, once per line at most: "MY. LIGHT. GIVE. BACK!"
8. **Simple yes and no:** "Uh-huh!", "Yah.", "Nuh-uh!", "Yay!", "Okay!", "Hmph."

## 3. Romance

**There is none.**
- No romance or affection system exists for Redcap in any plugin in this load order.
- Nothing tracks how much he likes the player. His dialogue is driven by quest stages and simple on/off switches: Dovahkaa toggle, armour unlocks, sweet roll count, Remi scene flags, Sanguine-met flag, and so on.
- His bond with the player is family and tribe: "You. Redcap's. Family."
- "Marry" comes up only as a joke in A Night to Remember: "You. Marry. THAT??" and "Friend...Ew."

## 5. Banter that already exists between followers

**What triggers it**
- Most of it plays on its own while both followers are following and near each other. The conditions are:
  - Both are in CurrentFollowerFaction.
  - They are within about 800-1100 units of each other.
  - The player is not sneaking.
  - Nobody is already in a scene or in dialogue.
  - A random-chance roll, and sometimes a day-of-week check, passes.
- The scenes run in a fixed order, each one unlocked by the stage or switch the previous scene set.
- Some scenes start from player dialogue instead, for example "Convince Xel to share his Flin".
