# INIGO — character sheet (banter-mod bible)

Source: read-only scan of the live load order (profile "DEEZ CS NUTZ - PICTA", 2026-10-18).
Inigo.esp alone has about 7,800 dialogue lines. Most of the facts below come from his lines and from the four in-game books:
- *Inigo's Journal*
- *Da Vinci's Journal*
- *In Search of a Champion I* and *II*

Where something is my reading rather than a direct quote, it is marked **(unsure)**.

## 1. Who he is

**Origin**
- He is a blue-furred Khajiit, born in a remote, very devout village in the Elsweyr desert. He has a golden twin brother, **Fergus**.
- In that village a blue baby is a "bad moon omen" and must be sacrificed.
- His birth mother **Atala** fled with the twins, left them in a shack with a note, and led the pursuers away. In the note she says a "fair but commanding" woman's voice told her to run.
- A soldier found the babies and took them to an orphanage in **Riverhold**, near the Cyrodiil border.

**Foster family**
- He and Fergus were adopted by two retired assassins: **Leah** (Khajiit, mother, archer) and **Da Vinci** (Argonian, father, swordsman). The couple named the boys after former guild-mates.
- Leah taught him the bow and Da Vinci taught the sword. He: "Apart from the nightly training sessions we were a normal family."
- Other children bullied him for his fur. Leah showed him "a handy trick with a rock and a glove".
- As teenagers the brothers killed a swarm of giant spiders that attacked Riverhold. This is where his spider obsession starts.
- When they left home, the parents gave Inigo Leah's bow **Lightning** and gave Fergus Da Vinci's sword **Thunder**.
- Da Vinci on Inigo as a boy: "That boy would joke on the gallows!"

**Brothers' adventuring years**
- They worked as sellswords all over Cyrodiil, often for free. Deeds include:
  - killing Blowaft the Flatulent, his first giant, which he did alone while Fergus slept
  - wiping out the Witches of Glamoor
  - killing Browulf the Broad
- Inigo calls those years "Those were happy times."

**Fergus's death**
- Twelve Khajiit-hating locals, who blamed the brothers for a run of robberies, raided their camp.
- Fergus, dying, pushed Da Vinci's sword into Inigo's hand and shoved him out the back of the tent: "My brother died but I live because of him."
- Inigo buried Fergus in a clearing past a ruined archway near Greenmead Cave, on the road to Skingrad.
- His parents later died guarding a trading caravan that bandits attacked. Langley found them "holding hands, heads resting brow to brow". Inigo: "I guess I am an orphan again."

**The fall**
- He joined a bandit gang and found **skooma** there.
- He fell for a bandit girl. He: "She robbed my heart then ate it in front of me" and "a vicious manipulative harpy".
- He left with an addict friend, **Felix**, and the two became mercenaries.
- Felix burst into his room one day and Inigo killed him, "instinct took over". Felix's voice then haunted him; the journal has the hallucinated arguments.
- **Lord Dupan** hired him and a partner to kill Dupan's two brothers, saying a lone survivor would get both shares. Sick from withdrawal and paranoid, Inigo shot his partner from cover.
- Dupan was then murdered by his sister, so no one was paid.
- Ashamed, he paid the guards of **Riften Jail** to lock him in Cell One so he could "repent". The guards robbed his belongings and joked about rent. He planned to die there.

**Key quest beats**
- **Meeting.** He believes the player is the partner he shot ("I would recognize your smell anywhere") and begs to be killed. The player recruits him instead: "Or die defending you! … It will be like old times."
- **The "shared past" twist.**
  - Langley questions his memory.
  - Later, on the "We need to talk" branch (InigoPJ… topics), Inigo admits the player was never that partner: "We never shared a past. I am ashamed."
  - He keeps the debt anyway: "I am still in your debt. You gave me a reason to go on."
  - Some players choose to keep the old story; there is a "you really had me going there" branch.
- **Langley.**
  - Langley Longseer is an eccentric old seer, painter and egg-collecting alchemist who lives in a cabin.
  - He has dreamed of **the Doom Strider**, a world-ending figure, and of **the Champion**: a blue Khajiit who escaped death as a baby. That is Inigo.
  - His failed "Summon Inigo" spell gave Inigo "mind vibrations"; that is the quest **Bad Vibrations** (InigoBadVibrations).
  - Langley can be prickly and rude to the player, and Inigo smooths it over: "He is quite a character. I believe he means well."
  - Inigo can move in with Langley if dismissed (LiveWithLangley topics).
- **Mr Dragonfly.**
  - In Windhelm, three brothers drugged him. Their witch "Mother" shrank him into a dragonfly in a jar.
  - Another jarred dragonfly, a man too long transformed to change back, saved him.
  - Inigo turned Mother into a moth and threw the jar off the Windhelm bridge; the brothers dived after it to their deaths.
  - He carries Mr Dragonfly in a jar and "hears" his thoughts. Since that change he can also sometimes understand horses, dogs and his own fleas.
- **Other reactive content:** main-quest commentary, Companions commentary, Peryite disease, a drunk night, the steed and horse-naming system, gifts, the player as vampire or werewolf, the Snow Point Beacon bandits, and an invisible-ink "Dear diary".

**Wants**
- To repay his debt to the player and earn forgiveness he doesn't think he has yet.
- To be "content, if not happy".
- To build a home in the Rift some day.
- His dream picture: two old battle-scarred friends sitting in the rain with their families, remembering.
- To find love, but "first I must feel worthy" (from the Kaidan banter).

**Fears and soft spots**
- Relapse: "my body does [want it], but my mind is made up".
- Being judged by his dead family: "What have I done with my life that is worthy of their pride?"
- Being left behind; there are many "you left me waiting… again" lines.
- Crypts, zombies and draugr: "if I suddenly scream do not hold it against me".
- He distrusts chickens and rabbits; this is a running joke.

**Values**
- Loyalty above all; he treats the player as family.
- Second chances: "you are not your past."
- No time for bullies: his fur is "a good idiot detector", a phrase from Fergus.
- He empathises with addicts: "sometimes choice ends when reaction begins."
- Prays to **Talos** now and then.

## 2. How he talks

**Person and grammar**
- Plain **first person**. He does **not** use the Khajiit third person ("this one") and refers to himself as "Inigo" only for a joke ("Silly Inigo" is mostly said *to* him).
- He mostly speaks **without contractions**: about 686 "I am" against about 296 "I'm" across the plugin (some "I'm" are Langley's). The same goes for "do not" and "let us".
- The tone is gentle, formal and slightly foreign, and very deadpan.

**What he calls people**
- **"my friend"** for the player, about 485 times. It is his signature.
- Later Kaidan calls him "brother", and Inigo answers "Yes brother?"
- No romantic pet names.

**Khajiit flavour**
- It comes through his **nose**, not his grammar ("smell" appears about 330 times):
  - "You smell hungry."
  - "You smell powerful."
  - "I would recognize your smell anywhere."
- Also fur, whiskers and tail, and his fleas: "My fleas keep me company when I am alone."

**Running jokes**
- Spiders. He loves killing them and wants a "spider smashing ceremony" at weddings.
- Sweetrolls ("Sweetrolls are irresistable").
- Mr Dragonfly's opinions, used to say what Inigo won't say directly.
- Chickens and rabbits are evil.
- Vanity ("Inigo the Humble, Brave and Handsome").
- His "steed".
- Writing to a diary that never answers.
- Silly rhymes and improvised songs, over spiders and over fights.
- Laughs are written "He he".

**What makes him soft**
- Fergus and his parents.
- Being thanked or praised by the player.
- Children.
- Quiet camp moments: "Fergus and I used to sit like this at the end of the day."

## 3. Romance with the player

- **There is no romance with the player in this install.** No romance plugin is installed: there is no "Inigo Romance" mod, and Inigo.esp has no relationship-rank variable.
- The player-facing arc is **friendship, debt and forgiveness**.
- **How the arc is tracked:**
  - **InigoPlayerJourney** (06CB85:Inigo.esp, stages 0–80) carries the debt / "shared past" talks.
  - **InigofollowerDialogue** (0012DF:Inigo.esp) holds the core follower state, including stage 30.
  - **InigoPlayerQuestions** (0C0C2E) and **InigoPlayerProg** (060164) hold the conversations the player starts.
- His own love life:
  - The bandit-girl heartbreak.
  - "The love of a good woman would be nice, but first I must feel worthy" (Kaidan banter).
  - "My lockpicking is like my love life - a bit hit and miss."
- **Jealousy:** none of the romantic kind.
  - He reacts warmly when the player marries: "I cannot believe you are married to Mjoll, you lucky devil… I am happy for you."
  - He jokes at the player's wedding: "You had a lovely wedding, but there was no additional spider smashing ceremony."
  - The only "jealous" lines are about others envying *him*: "Your magical friend is jealous of our friendship" (summons) and "He is just jealous of our friendship" (Langley).

**Inigo's side of the existing banter, by follower**
