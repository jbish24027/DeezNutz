## 1. Story summary, in order

### The cast (as the text shows them)

- **The player ("rookie")** - a Witch Hunter. Carries a blood curse inherited from Felix. Ozwald spared them because Felix "still lives in them". Something keeps healing their taint. The Black Owl and Ja'zel both treat them as the "Chick" of a long plan.
- **Ozwald** (UA 0083AD) - senior Witch Hunter, the player's partner. Serious, dutiful, never complains. Breton. Raised by Ja'zel, hates him for it. Lost Felix, his old partner, and a sister. Dreams of opening a Breton-food inn. Dies in Act 3.
  - Variants: mutated Ozwald boss (UA 11E071), dying Ozwald (UA 11E0F0).
- **Felix** - Ozwald's dead former partner. The player inherited his curse. Appears as a ghost in the replay hub ("I am Laza... and so are you").
- **Ja'zel, the Chick Trader** (UA 006F0B) - Khajiit master of the Witch Hunters. Talks in "yes? no? hmm?". Smiling, cruel, manipulative.
  - He set up the Ebonwall massacre to harvest a heart stone.
  - He used the hunters as "a nursery for the curse".
  - Ja'cobee, the real last Chick Trader, says Ja'zel is an impostor.
  - He calls the player his beloved "Chick" and dies in Hinnom.
  - Other forms: dream Ja'zel (UA 064926), final boss (UA 152431), dying Ja'zel (UA 15254C).
- **Eyes of Hinnom** - Ja'zel's partner. A spy and assassin "inside the wall". Killed in In Plain Sight.
- **FL-4N, "DweGear"** (UA 009DC7) - Dwemer spider automaton. Scripts call it "Yatsuhashi". Calls itself the player's "senior" and the player "rookie". Cheerful, "safety first", says "Snap!" and "Spunk!". Calls Romion "the professor" or "the doctor".
- **Romion** (UA 008826) - workshop engineer and airship pilot. Former Psijic who left "for lack of talent". A lore nerd and gentle worrier. Plans the escape to Artaeum.
  - RomionHolo (UA 017282) is his hologram in Quagmire.
- **Cedric** (UA 0147BF) - veteran hunter, the oldest next to Ozwald. Easygoing survivor: "the adaptable ones live longest". In Act 3 he hunts the player on Ja'zel's orders.
- **The Chaplain** (UA 008820) - a ghostly figure who appears with a bell sound. Preaches: "O tainted soul... we are all nothing but lice... partake in Kyne's Feast of Love."
  - The replay hub names this voice "Research Assistant Boltzmer". Boltzmer is Yelem's assistant in the dream "Louse". So the Chaplain is probably Boltzmer (unsure).
- **The Black Owl** - main figures: Owl (UA 012C70), OwlDog (UA 015289), Black (UA 014B4E), BO in Act 3 (UA 1329BB).
  - A "kind" helper who keeps offering deals. Says "I am always on your side."
  - Admits the player "agreed" to a contract.
  - In the dream "Confined" he is shown writing the whole plot: "a puppet and a patsy... a Chick Trader and hunting witches... Fair is foul. I will be the kind one."
  - Ja'zel senses "someone without a name, not Sheogorath".
  - He is the real puppet-master. His true identity is unsure.
- **Jhunal, the White Owl** (UA 006EDD) - Lalanoah's pet owl. He is really the old Nordic god of runes, Jhunal.
  - After Oneiromancer he vanishes, and everyone except the player forgets him.
  - Kalka says he hid himself and the land's blessing will fade.
- **Orlando / Orland** (UA 014C27; courier UA 02C263; UA 4863F3 in Skip Hinnom) - old curio-shop owner.
  - Says he is "Orlando the Knowledgeable", a "kind mage from Atmora".
  - Appears in dreams across eras. He sews Ja'zel's head back on. He gives the Saarthal boy the mask that turns him into the Gray Owl.
  - Says he knows what will happen to the player.
  - He is probably an owl-faction agent or another face of the Owl (unsure).
- **Lalanoah** (UA 006F14) - bright, brave little girl in the Blessed Glen near Falkreath. Healer family.
  - Mother: Laelnoah, kidnapped by the Holy Pipers. Father: Gerhard, an Imperial soldier, died "in some sort of sacrifice".
  - She is the "blue bird" and the Red Drop's true vessel. The Drop "prefers her blood".
  - Her family line goes back to Hamah, a Daughter of Kyne. People around this line love them "as their own".
  - Late forms: wounded Lalanoah (UA 11E0AD), "LalanoahSack" (UA 123309).
- **Brandt** (UA 006F0A) - Nord, Lalanoah's guardian, friend of Gerhard. Drinks, says "true Nord" constantly, soft-hearted. Killed by mutated Ozwald in Little Red.
- **Goroll / "Groll"** (UA 006EE2) - rock troll who cooks for the house. Lost a daughter.
- **Kalka** (UA 0085B5) - spore creature in the house: "my spores", "my people were burned". Old guardian of "Hamah".
- **Pets** - Skoll the dog (UA 006ED8) and Kanra (UA 007711).
- **Cerebline, "the Aunt"** (UA 0172ED) - Altmer from the Temple of Kynareth. Claims to be the sister of Lalanoah's grandmother. Nurses Lalanoah, then breaks down.
- **Arkved** - the "Bronze Key". The Thalmor kept his brain and spinal cord in a tank. He is linked to Quagmire, and in Quagmire he acts as a guide.
- **Zuzu the Oneiromancer** - giant maiden-witch of Vaermina, daughter of Ysgramor's "Dumb Old Giant" counselor. She hid her past. In truth she shielded the hunters.
- **Hela / "Hera"** (UA 0171A1 dream; Witch UA 012D04) - witch of Vahdin Holt. Calls herself a priestess of Kyne but is pledged to Hircine.
- **The Holy Pipers and the Sacred Anatomancer** - extremists cast out of the Vigil of Stendarr. Boss forms: UA 0CB1CA, 0D0441, 0D0442. See the VIGILANT links below.

### Links to the earlier mod VIGILANT

- **Holy Pipers.** They were the Vigil of Stendarr's inquisitorial branch, gone rotten. They torture and kidnap suspected Daedra worshippers. Stendarr cursed them, and they wear "juicer" headgear to hold the curse down.
  - Ozwald: "Must have been the work of Molag Bal. He treats Vigilants of Stendarr like his toys."
- **The Sacred Anatomancer** is a VIGILANT character. Vigilant.esm has the side quest zzzAoMSubQ02 "Sacred Anatomancer", with a good path (kill him) and an evil path. It also has a cell "Piper Temple" and a quest "GLENMORIL: Delete Anatomancer" (4EFDFB:Vigilant.esm).
  - Romion's briefing gives about ten versions of his past, and the papers keep rewriting themselves. They mention the cursed mansion in Chorrol, Lake Illinalta, a Khajiit called Ja'zhan, Stendarr's Beacon, the Dawnguard, the Ratway, and even "he was the Dragonborn".
  - These read like echoes of the VIGILANT hero's possible paths. That is a strong hook for anyone who played VIGILANT (my reading, unsure).
- **Laza the Shepherd and Lamae.** The curse on Felix and the player is "Laza: the shepherd trapped in the past, longing for what can't be there anymore". Laza survived the nomads Lamae attacked. Ja'zel asks: "Was Molag Bal not the only one who was there?" Vigilant has Laza boss quests (e.g. "VS Laza 02").
- **Talos.** Ja'zel calls Talos a "cannibal, demon of Alcaire" and envies him. Talos-worship themes also run through Ysmir's Howl.

### zzzGHMq04 "The Bronze Key" - Ysmir's Howl

1. FL-4N fetches the rookie for a new mission.
2. At the HQ (the Abandoned Chapel of Julianos, called "the workshop/temple") the Beast of Haafingar lies dead. Cedric brings the Chick Trader's orders: steal a "key" from a Thalmor site in Ysmir's Howl, the Temple City of Talos southwest of Solitude.
3. Optional eavesdrop. Cedric asks Ozwald why he spared the rookie. Ozwald admits: "They inherited the curse from Felix... I wanted to keep them near." Cedric warns him the rookie is not Felix.
4. On the airship, Romion's briefing:
   - The city was "wiped out by an epidemic". More likely a massacre and beast-possession experiments by the Eton Nir Research Institute.
   - Its chief is Alfilion: ex-assassin, Cloud Ruler Temple veteran, makes masks from his victims' skulls.
   - "Skeeven" (Rieklings mutated with skeever blood) fill the sewers.
   - First Council era Dwemer automatons.
   - The "Bronze Key" is Thalmor jargon for a person: the body of the mage Arkved, used in Vaermina research.
5. Landing at dusk. Ja'zel watches from a cliff. You sneak through the sewers, or skip them by the bridge (stage 100 path).
   - The Chaplain appears in the flooded lower sewer.
   - FL-4N waits outside, caught "playing with fire".
   - Thalmor radio chatter tracks your progress (zzzGHMq04Radio).
6. You enter the Council Hall through the kitchen pantry. Bosses: Brass Gear Queen, then Chief Justiciar Alfilion.
7. In the Dream Engine Room you find Arkved: a brain and spinal cord in a tank, beside the dead giant Zuzu, the Oneiromancer.
   - Ja'zel: her soul lives in Quagmire and warns witches of hunters. That is why the elder witches and the Red Drop can't be found.
   - Only the player can take the Key.
8. Taking Arkved makes the Oneiromancer try to "burn out" the player's mind.
   - You black out into a Black Owl dream (zzzGHMq04BO). The Owl talks to an invisible Arkved, the "guide in Quagmire".
   - You wake on the airship. Ozwald: "a miracle you're even alive... the Chick Trader seems aware of it."
   - The hunters blew up the lab and the city.

### zzzGHMq04Sub01 "Shop of Curiosities" - Falkreath / Blessed Glen

1. Lalanoah, Brandt and Goroll take you to Orlando's travelling curio shop.
   - Brandt knew Orlando as a kid. The player may remember Orlando from Markarth.
   - Stock: the "sword of St. Jiub", a Sheogorath vase, a Dwemer golden sweet roll.
2. Lalanoah finds her mother's staff, with its gem missing. Orlando bought it from "men in strange helmets near Helgen". He says they went to Piper's Hill, past the Jerall Mountains in Bruma territory.
3. Brandt asks the Jarl of Falkreath for a search party. Many volunteers had kin taken by the Pipers.
4. You walk Lalanoah home. She chatters about her mom beating up a drunk Brandt with that staff.
5. The Black Owl appears by the house (stage 90):
   - "You've met Zuzu. She rejected you."
   - He offers a key to "another door at the bottom of the well": "Sleep in the starving darkness... someone else will break your curse."
   - This looks like an early opt-out or bad ending. The well cell is "Old Well". Unsure exactly what happens.

### zzzGHMq05 "Oneiromancer" - Quagmire (Vaermina's realm)

1. Briefing in the Analysis Room. Romion opens a Dwemer "gate" from Arkved's link to Quagmire, so you go in in the flesh.
   - Quagmire changes bodies and minds.
   - Ja'zel supplies "tuning boxes" with five chicks inside, run by the stillborn baby from the Cradle Woods: "a curse given flesh". Romion and Ozwald hate it.
   - Arkved agreed to guide them for free: "he already got the payment".
2. The Chaplain appears. You enter the gate and get split from Ozwald.
3. In Quagmire you head for Arkved's Tower. Optional dream rooms on the way (Sub01-Sub07, below). Test subjects babble about the Blue Star, Yelem and Hamah's blood (zzzGHMq05Subject).
4. You rejoin Ozwald. Ozwald: "Had a nice dream?" Fight in the Oneiromancer's Demesne: Boss01, then Boss02 and Boss03.
5. Romion's hologram has Ozwald work a Dwemer vanguard ship's console to pull out Zuzu's memories.
6. When you touch the memory you are pulled into a strange library (Jhunal's), alone.
   - Hela says Zuzu is gone and curses "the irreverent who approach the forbidden" in Jhunal's name.
   - Something drags you around.
   - A friendly dog (OwlDog: "I'm on your side... because I'm a kind person") guides you.
   - You beat the "Forgotten Vessel" (the Jhunal boss) and touch the rune stone.
7. You land in the Room of Love (zzzGHMq05Charm). Ghost voices confess they love Lalanoah, or her mother or grandmother, "as their own": Brandt, Goroll, Gerhard, a slave trader, the Altmer aunt, and a hanged man who loved Hamah.
8. The Black Owl:
   - "The secret has been revealed."
   - "You should have simply let the orphan die... you agreed to it."
   - The "contract" can't be undone, but you could break it from your side.
9. You wake a week later (168 hours) in Falkreath.

### zzzGHMq05Joint "Broken Egg" - Falkreath

1. Lalanoah finds you sleeping where a statue used to be. Brandt says there was never a statue.
2. Jhunal the owl has been gone a week. You help search. Brandt is now raising Lalanoah "like a true Nord", with hunting practice.
3. In the Cockatrice Lair (the old "big bird" nest) a mutated, babbling Witch Hunter Ulrik attacks ("Found it! Want it!"). Brandt sends Lalanoah home. You kill Ulrik. This matters later.

### zzzGHMq06 "Or the Gospel" - Vahdin Holt

1. Ozwald turns up at Lalanoah's with the airship. He showed her the player's sketch. "Her presence was full of Magicka."
2. On board, Ozwald confides:
   - In Quagmire he remembered that the Oneiromancer protected them.
   - The Chick Trader used them "as a nursery for the curse".
   - Romion is talking to a friend in High Rock about escaping.
   - They still need a Red Drop first.
3. You sleep in the airship bed. The Chaplain appears. You arrive at Vahdin Holt, Kyne's old forest temple.
4. Ja'zel's plan at camp:
   - Enemies: Goji clan "Reachmen" (really an old Nord clan), Ruskahr orc archers who worship the White Owl, Hircine-summoned atronachs and Karstaag-kin, a cockatrice.
   - Ja'zel's heart stone from Ebonwall will break the barrier.
   - Ozwald realises Ja'zel arranged the Ebonwall massacre. Ja'zel: "the less involved, the better."
5. You fire the heart stone. You break four barricades with automaton allies, a Dwemer knight and archer, and a spider queen. The sky turns blood-moon.
6. Cedric's unit is wiped out. You kill the avatar of Hircine in the Sanctuary. Hela's spirit fades.
7. Deeper in, there is no Red Drop, only an inscription naming "the thief and his family". The treasure "walks around in a different form". Ja'zel stays to decode it.
8. Back at the workshop, Romion's test results:
   - Ozwald has about three months left.
   - The rookie is worse, "but something is healing the taint".
   - Ozwald: if the curse isn't broken, "you'll have to kill me."

### zzzGHmq06Sub01 "St. Bazura" - Blessed Glen

1. Orlando, now a "courier", hands you Lalanoah's letter. He knows "most of what will happen to you". About the Black Owl: "if he gives you a book, don't ever open it."
2. Party at Lalanoah's for the made-up Falkreath saint Bazura. Bazura was the Hero of Cyrodiil's follower who set himself on fire as a decoy, then rose after three days. Or it's just "By Azura".
3. You light the effigy.
4. Nobody remembers Jhunal except Kalka, who says he hid on purpose.
   - Cerebline the Aunt visited. Lalanoah will apprentice at the Temple of Kynareth.
   - Brandt wants a quiet family life, with a romance joke option.
   - Kalka: "she'll be a suitable vessel for succession."
5. Lalanoah admits she fears waking up alone. She knows her father died.
6. FL-4N arrives through a door it "never noticed before": the inscription is decoded, last mission.

### zzzLaMMq01 "The Pipers" - Piper's Hill (start of Act 3)

1. Airship briefing:
   - The target is Laelnoah, Lalanoah's mother, a descendant of the inscription's family, held by the Holy Pipers.
   - Ozwald and the player both know Lalanoah. Romion smells a trap.
   - The Red Drop is Shor's spilled heart-blood from Atmora, maybe a meteorite.
   - Plan: take the Drop to the Psijics on Artaeum via Alcaire, and betray Ja'zel.
   - Ozwald's inn dream.
2. Piper's Hill appeared out of nowhere a few years ago, and the road out has collapsed.
3. The Chaplain preaches again, and Ozwald snaps at him.
4. Four Anatomancer apparitions spout "Love, I and We" riddles. One knows Ozwald's name.
5. Boss chain in the Pipers' Temple: Sacred Anatomancer, then Arcana of Anatomancy, then Radiance Seeker.
   - Dying: "my flower is wilting... but yours will do nicely."
   - The player gains a Blood Curse spell at stage 120.

### zzzLaMMq02 "In Plain Sight" - Piper's Hill

1. Laelnoah is dead: throat cut, an eye stuffed in the wound, gutted. "Too much for one person's organs."
2. The Red Drop in her is grey and powerless. Ozwald guesses the awful answer: fill it with blood.
3. A shadow (the Eyes of Hinnom) speaks: "The Drop prefers... her daughter's blood. Let us kill her."
4. You chase and kill the Eyes of Hinnom in three fights so Ja'zel never learns of the betrayal.
5. FL-4N says three days passed and the player hasn't eaten.
6. While calling the airship, Ozwald realises: "We've lost. There's nowhere to run." A sniper ("revenge for Ulrik") shoots him in the head.
7. You kill the sniper in the watchtower. Ozwald's corpse sprouts tentacles and flies off as a monster. FL-4N finds a small metal insect on him, the same thing found in Felix's bed.

### zzzLaMMq03 "Falling Sky" - Airship Deck

1. Storm. Romion is breaking down. FL-4N keeps the bug secret from him. You rest.
2. Thalmor airships attack, revenge for the Temple City. Someone leaked your position.
3. You fight on the deck with a Dwemer ally, blow two Thalmor engines (15 and 10 second escapes), and kill two automatons.
4. A beam hits. The airship falls, and you land near Lalanoah's home.

### zzzLaMMq04 "Little Red" - Blessed Glen and the Abandoned Chapel of Julianos

1. The Blessed Glen is dark and Lalanoah's family is gone. Brandt is dying: "I cut off that monster's arm... take care of Lalanoah... to Sovngarde." Mutated Ozwald fights you, then flies away.
2. You are knocked out (an arrow hit). Ja'zel "rushed from Alcaire":
   - He hired a healer, Cerebline.
   - He burned the family's "contaminated" bodies.
   - Lalanoah is all but a hostage.
3. Hunt Ozwald. Dying, he thinks you are Felix and hands over the Red Drop: "I found the Red Drop at last."
   - Dialogue choice: "you'll be human to the end" or "we were monsters all along".
   - He asks you to end it.
4. At the Chapel, Ja'zel shows off a dead Psijic from Alcaire, so the escape route is gone. He makes two pills: "One for you. The other? Do with it what you wish."
5. Lalanoah is now kept in a sack, her body "falling apart". The Aunt is going mad.
6. Ending choice:
   - Give the pill to Lalanoah (stage 150). She dies. The quest moves to Lice and Maggots.
   - Drink it yourself (stage 145). This starts zzzLaMMqAlt.
   - Either way she ends up dead. The "antidote" is poison: the curse lifts on death.

### zzzLaMMq05 "Lice and Maggots" - Great Hole of Hinnom

1. The Black Owl appears:
   - "Why don't we make a happy ending together?"
   - The pill was "only poison".
   - Your curse fades with a peaceful life, but you're "bloodthirsty". "It never had to be you."
2. Cedric and hunters ambush you. Ja'zel told them you killed Ozwald for the antidote. You can persuade Cedric (stage 35: he leaves Skyrim with the others) or kill him (stage 40/45).
3. Ja'zel opens Hinnom. You become an enemy of the Chick Trader and Witch Hunter factions.
4. The descent:
   - Yelem's Sixth Laboratory
   - Kanin Barrow
   - Chick Orphanage
   - the Talos room, where you see a fake Lalanoah and three "chick bombs" blow out the floor
   - the Great Hole
5. Ja'zel's sermons: Ysgramor chased a hare into the depths, Yelem chased an owl. "Ja'zel wants to be a Great Cat." "Let us fall into Heaven together."
6. Boss: Ja'zel. You fall again to the Bottom of Hinnom.
7. Ja'zel lies dying: "Ja'zel loved you from the moment he met you... the real Chick." Optional: finish him (stage 145). Otherwise he dies anyway (stage 149).
8. Leads to zzzRevMq00 "Departure" (Act 4).

### zzzLaMMqAlt "Skip Hinnmon" - alternate path

1. You drink the pill and collapse.
2. You are dragged through the Secret Sanctuary. Ja'zel, Orlando and Torvesard (UA 4863EB) are there.
3. Ja'zel: "Ja'zel alone will love you... Khajiit will put her remains in your pocket... Your story lies out there, beyond the sea of ink."
4. You fall to the bottom of Hinnom, find Ja'zel's remains and read his letter.
5. Leads to zzzRevMq00 "Departure". LaMMq05 is skipped.

### Dream side-quests in Quagmire (during Oneiromancer)

These are optional memory rooms. Each gives "Mnemoli" points.

- **Sub01 "Dream Within a Dream"** (Anequina mausoleum)
  - Child Ozwald and child Felix.
  - Ja'zel's twin Ja'zeri is beheaded. Orlando sews Ja'zel's head on and tells "the story of the Chick Traders".
  - Chick ghosts sing "Rolly-Polly": "You are the sixth EGG... you must turn the Wheel."
  - Laza's little sister and father (Laza's family tent).
- **Sub02 "The Last Chick Trader"** - Ja'cobee, prince of Anequina, the last true Chick Trader.
  - He crushed the EGG.
  - He warns: "be wary of the Owl... observing from the very beginning."
  - Ja'zel is an impostor.
  - With him is Ulliss, a lost girl with a tail who "is a Khajiit now".
- **Sub03 "The Night of Tears"** - Saarthal. Orlando, the "kind mage from Atmora", gives an orphan boy a mask, and he becomes the Gray Owl.
- **Sub04 "Louse"** - Professor Yelem and his assistant Boltzmer. "We lost the ability to sleep." They wait for "the Blue Orphan who stands on Mnemo-Li".
- **Sub05 "The Owls"** - the Black and Gray Owls chat. Gray is nurturing a tree branch from Skyrim into "a good bard".
- **Sub06 "Confined"** - the Owl, contained, writes the player's story outline.
- **Sub07 "Lucid Dream"** - Ja'zel and two hunters (one is Ulrik) plan the past.
  - Flush a cursed baby down the river so kind Anem picks it up and it defiles the Nedic god's statue under Falkreath.
  - Turn dying Felix into a sacrifice with Yelem's mutagen.
  - Arrive late to Falkreath's "six chicks" so the real Chick (the player) slips in.
  - Ja'zel realises "it is the tale of the one who watches Ja'zel now."
  - The player is looped back to start each scene.
- **Subject** - mutated test subjects. Dialogue only.

### Replays (zzzGHReplay*)

- At the HQ you "Sleep inside the EGG" to re-fight a past mission's bosses.
- One replay each: Mq02 Ebonwall, Mq03 Tainted Orphan, Mq04 Bronze Key, Mq05 Oneiromancer, Mq06 Or the Gospel, Mq07 The Pipers.
- You are teleported in and sent back to a return marker at the end. Solo.
- zzzGHReplayGen is the hub. Felix's ghost ("I am Laza... and so are you") and Boltzmer's voice are there.
