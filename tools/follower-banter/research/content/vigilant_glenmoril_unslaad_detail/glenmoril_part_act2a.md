# GLENMORIL - Act 2, part A (The Blue Bird -> A Thousand Daggers)

Sources: dlgc.txt (lines 2-946, 2047-2110), glen_story.txt, q_Glenmoril.txt, plus live record reads (script property targets resolved to their cells and worldspaces).
All FormIDs are `XXXXXX:Glenmoril.esm` unless marked Skyrim.esm.

Act 2 is "Witch Hunter". Act 1 (the "Lrh" quests, Lalanoah's family) ends in zzzLrhMq10. Its stage 225 (or 999) starts zzzGHMq01. zzzGHMq01 stage 0 sets the act counter to 2.

## 1. Story summary

### The Blue Bird (zzzGHMq01)
- Three game days after Act 1, Lalanoah's mother's white owl, Jhunal, comes home. Lalanoah is thrilled and thinks her mom will be back soon.
- Uncle Brandt waits until Lalanoah is asleep. Over a drink at midnight he admits she cries at night for her parents. He asks the player to find her father, Gerhard, a Nord soldier in the Legion. He gives 100 gold "war funds" for bribes.
- The white owl talks to the player, and only to the player. It warns: do not look for her father. Her parents "don't have long to live."
- Quartermaster Agrippa in Dragon Bridge is a snob who wants a bribe (or a persuade). Gerhard's unit was sent to Fort Sungard against the Forsworn. They vanished on the way to Bilegulch Mine. The Legion calls them deserters. Many units have vanished there. A Khajiit "Chick Trader" asked the same questions earlier.
- Near Fort Sungard the player finds Ja'zel, the Chick Trader. He is a child slaver from Act 1. He jokes about selling Lalanoah "two or three chicks at a discount". He waits outside a ghoul cave.
- Inside, Gerhard is dying. A "giant blue bird" ate his unit. He gives his sword for his family and asks the player to find a hair ornament he bought for his daughter.
- The cave opens into a dream-like realm (worldspace "Sanctuary of Namira"). The blue bird is Felix, a mutated Witch Hunter. He thinks the player is his partner Ozwald and talks about a sister "who smiles like the sun and loves white flowers". Boss fight.
- After Felix dies, the Black Owl (a dark twin of Jhunal) holds a "sacrament". It hands the player a chalice. The player is cursed with the Blood Curse.
- Ozwald arrives with a Dwemer rifle. He shoots the Black Owl, then shoots the player.
- The player wakes in Romion's Workshop, under the Abandoned Chapel of Julianos. This is the Witch Hunter HQ. Ozwald and Romion argue over the player. The curse should have turned the player into a ghoul, but "something seems to be restraining" it.
- CHOICE: join the witch hunt, or die. Refuse once and Ozwald hands over a threatening letter from Ja'zel. It names three people Ja'zel will "collect your debt" from (see section 4). Refuse again and Ozwald and Romion attack. Then a bad ending (Ja'zel's "Ithelia" call) quits to the main menu.
- After joining, the player meets FL-4N, a chatty Dwemer automaton who insists on being the player's "senior". The player brings the sword to Brandt and the hair ornament to Lalanoah. The player can lie that her daddy is "fine". Brandt tells the Jarl, and Gerhard's honor is cleared.
- The white owl says the only way to break the curse is to find "the untainted daughter of Kyne". The player must stay "pure", avoid battle and avoid passion. Then it vanishes.

### Ebonwall (zzzGHMq02)
- FL-4N tracks the player down using a transmitter that Romion secretly put in the player's body. Ja'zel demanded it as the price for not executing the player.
- At the chapel, Ja'zel gives the order. Ashlanders rioted in Wulfharth's Ebonwall, a fortress town on the Morrowind border. Their Wise Woman, Tabiah, is a Glenmoril witch. Kill her and take her Red Drop. Ozwald warns: never look at Ja'zel's silent partner, the wall-crawling "bighead".
- They fly in Romion's airship. During the briefing they talk about Ashkhan Gadaratto (rumored child of the Nerevarine), Magni the Iron Fist (the Nord militia leader who "never takes captives"), and Tabiah, a former lady of Almalexia.
- The drop goes into the Ebonwall worldspace. There are piles of slaughtered Ashlanders. The Chaplain sings a creepy warning. The player fights a Gulakhan in the watchtower. Horns sound as the Nord volunteer army arrives.
- Thalmor agents from the Eton Nir Research Institute (Romion's old employer) are also hunting the witch's curse. After their commander dies, the player can prank-call the Thalmor on a soul-gem communicator. Options: "I'm the Dragonborn", "I'm Nazeem" (the Thalmor then really kill Nazeem in Whiterun), "I'm Delphine". FL-4N tries to trace the call while Ozwald grumbles.
- At the graveyard the player fights two Gulakhans, then the Ashkhan. In his second form he is powered by Boethiah, and a storm breaks.
- Under the Boethiah statue, in the Shrine of Boethiah, Tabiah sits by a coffin, mad from a magic "heart stone". She murmurs "No one will take these children away". Ozwald decides she is "as good as dead". They take the heart stone, which goes to Ja'zel. Killing her is optional.
- Black Owl dream (Svartibrunnr): "Cursed? No, this is a blessing." "Lose yourself in rage."
- Endings:
  - GOOD: kill Magni (optional) AND spare Tabiah. The Nord army pulls back and survivors resettle in Windhelm's Gray Quarter. This unlocks "Embers".
  - BAD: anything else. "All Ashlanders executed. There were no survivors."

### Embers (zzzGHMq02GoodEnd), good ending only
- FL-4N reluctantly says survivors went to Windhelm. Tabiah sits catatonic in the New Gnisis Cornerclub, cared for by Ambarys Rendar.
- Outside, among dead Ashlanders, a Dunmer child, Cruviah, speaks broken Dunmeri. The player leads Cruviah to Tabiah ("alma" means mother). The child says: "Alma no cry. Os happy. Thanks."

### Percussive Maintenance (zzzGHMq02Sub01)
- Brandt "hit it a little" and broke Lalanoah's precious music box. She has locked herself in her room.
- Romion recognises it as a rare Yelem School piece and fixes it happily. Ozwald drops by, looking for the Black Horse Courier newspaper.
- When the player gets back, Lalanoah has run away and left a note. She is at the Crypts of Kyne, talking to Kalka, a mushroom creature. Kalka calls her "Ms. Hamah". This hints at a hidden identity (unsure). The Ending Owl later mentions "the secrets of Hamah and her bloodline".
- She comes home. Groll serves soup and a sweet roll. Brandt has drunk himself to sleep. The white owl scolds the player: "The smell of blood around you is even stronger now."

### Tainted Orphan (zzzGHMq03)
- Ja'zel summons the player alone to a slaughterhouse under the Warrens in Markarth. A captive is reciting an Alessian Order poem, and Ja'zel muses "Molag Bal plays a strange game...". Ja'zel orders the player to kill a "slacker": a fellow Chick Trader who broke the traders' rule over "that prostitute Maria" and a cursed child she bore. A butcher then collects "cat and bear meat" and asks for "baby goat" next time. Ja'zel also says the children taken from Markarth work in the mines.
- Mission: in the Cradle Woods, find "the rotten stillborn baby, this delicate orphan". It is the source of a ghoul plague. Romion has lost his monocle of Julianos, probably when "a large bird" attacked the airship. During the briefing they cover the Na clan of Reachmen, ghosts of Emperor Cuhlecain's loyalists, Glenmoril witch Anem (who leads baby sacrifices), and ghoul-making worm parasites. Romion, an elf, calls Talos a cold-hearted traitor. Ozwald pushes back.
- The landing cave is flooded, so they swim. In the fog Ozwald goes missing. The Chaplain sings again. A ghost, Artur, still believes "Hjalti" will come back for him.
- Fights: Botvid (ghost rebel leader) at the Rebel Fort, then again in the Cave of Rebirth. Thalmor corpses lie about. Artur hails the monster baby as "Cuhlecain's successor". He fights the player, then the witch Anem fights in two forms.
- Ja'zel teleports in and takes the crying baby: "It is adorable how it cries for its mother, no?" Mission over.
- Black Owl dream 03: the Owl dotes on "a good baby... This child is empty".
- Back at HQ Romion gives a medical check with parasite killer. No mutation yet, and their memories are still clear.

### A Thousand Daggers (zzzGHMq03Sub01)
- A comedy break. Brandt dodges Lalanoah with apple pie and drags the player on a 10,000-gold bounty. The target is a man who mass-produces cheap iron daggers. This is a meme: the player can rage, "Are you saying making lots of iron daggers is bad?!"
- Brandt wants the gold to send Lalanoah to a magic school in the Imperial City. He has "something bad" against Winterhold College.
- The "factory" is in Brandt's childhood hideout cave. The bounty, Kodaavkiin, a bear-man, is secretly smuggling for the Thalmor ("Glory to the Thalmor!"). After his defeat he blows up the forge.
- The player is knocked out by rocks and wakes in Lalanoah's house. There is no prize. Lalanoah decides she will go to the Temple of Kynareth instead, so she can stay close to everyone.
- The owl asks why the player keeps fighting: "to protect", "to gain" or "because it feels good". On the "daughter of Kyne" it says: "One was tainted by you."

### To Dust (zzzGHEndingOwl), hidden or loop ending
- This takes place in the "Old Well" cell, the mod's debug and chapter-skip room. A third owl warns: "You're back again... How many times do we have to repeat the same ending... your fateline will be anchored." It also warns about "him" (most likely Ja'zel; unsure) and "the secrets of Hamah and her bloodline".
- If the player lingers, Jhunal catches them. The player is dragged through the dark while the candles go out, then the game quits to the main menu. It saves meta flags ("EndJhunal", "TimeBreach") to a JSON file, so the mod remembers it across saves. The exact trigger is unsure; it is set up by trigger boxes in the Old Well.
