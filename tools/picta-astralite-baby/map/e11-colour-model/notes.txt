HELPERS (Effects11.cpp 513-545)
 Curve(c,p)=max(c,0)^p per channel; Desat(c,d)=max(lerp(c,mean3(c),d),0) (mean, not luma; d<0 = saturation boost);
 ColorFilter(c,F,a)=lerp(c,1,a)*F  -> a=0: multiply; a=1: record hue REPLACED by F.

   false: sky = VParams*(BlendColor0*v.x+BlendColor1*v.y+BlendColor2*v.z) (vanilla dome vertex weights, all three bands)

   then lerp(sky, lerp(top*0.5, that, earthShadow(ShadowAmount)), aboveHorizon)
