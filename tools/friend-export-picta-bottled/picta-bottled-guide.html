The idea

No ENB at all

Picta is made for real ENB. Here it runs through Bottled Shaders instead, which reads ENB presets with its built-in Effects 11. Picta's core shader files are locked (encrypted), so a small helper, ENB Extender 1.6.0, unlocks and compiles them for Bottled. ENB's own files are never installed.

1 · WEATHERSPicta pluginWeather and climate records, Picta's skies and clouds.
2 · UNLOCKKiLoader + ExtenderCompiles the locked Silent Horizons 2 shader core.
3 · RENDERBottled Effects 11Reads Picta's preset and applies it per weather.
4 · FINISHReShadeNorthman II with my lane preset, depth of field on top.

Step by step

Putting it together

Close MO2 and the game before every change to your lists. Tick things off as you go; the page remembers your ticks in this browser.

  1. Turn real ENB and the old weather setup off

    Disable the mods, keep the folders. This is a switch, not an uninstall.

  2. Install the stock downloads and my mods

    Use the folder names exactly as above. Then place each one in the lane order.

  3. Place the plugins by hand

    Never run LOOT on Authoria. Put each plugin directly after the one named here, and keep plugins.txt, loadorder.txt and the plugin groups in step.

    PluginGoes right after
    Picta - Weathers of Aurbis.esmVweaponsALL.esl
    Picta Aurbis - Nordic Winds - Patch.espNordic Winds.esp
    Picta - Weathers of Skyrim.espMists of Tamriel.esp
    Authoria - Picta over NAT.espPicta - Weathers of Skyrim.esp
    Patch - Picta 1.1.1 - Darkened to 1.0.1.espAuthoria - Picta over NAT.esp
    Picta - Lok Vah Koor patch.espReverb Interior Sounds - Astralite.esp
    Patch - Picta Clear Skies x Requiem.espPicta - Lok Vah Koor patch.esp
    Patch - Picta Seasonal.espPatch - Picta Clear Skies x Requiem.esp

    Only one full-slot plugin is added (the Picta .esm). Everything else is light-flagged.

  4. Launch once, then copy my Community Shaders settings

    Start the game, reach the main menu, quit. Bottled writes a fresh settings file. Then change these values inside it. Don't paste my whole settings file over yours; it carries machine-specific choices.

    • Turned off at boot: HiZ Occlusion, Fur Shells, Hair Backlighting, HDR Display, Reverse Z.
    • Screen Space GI: off outdoors, on indoors only. Outdoors it gives a colour wash. I use a small interior-only scene file for this (I send it). Never press "Save Settings" while standing indoors, or it saves GI on everywhere.
    • Light Limit Fix: contact shadows on.
    • Shader compiler threads: 16 / 4.
    • Snow Cover: copy my values.

  5. First real launch

    The first launch compiles every shader and takes a few minutes. KiLoader may close the game once to set itself up. That's normal; start it again.

    Then go outside in Skyrim proper (Whiterun tundra is a good spot) before judging anything.

  6. Check the log

    Open BottledShaders.log in Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE.

    • Good: four lines "Loaded encrypted FX file through ENB Extender", "Preset loaded from …Data\enbseries", "Loaded raindrop texture", and "Loaded location weather for 1 worldspaces".
    • Bad: "requires KiLoader and ENB Extender", "ENB Extender compile failed", "No preset in use", or "Unable to open file". That last one means the game-folder copies didn't deploy: check Root Builder, the Shader Core and the Root twin.
    • Harmless: "Effect file not found" for depth of field or lens (when you skip my DOF), and SMAA texture warnings.
What makes it mine

The tuning on top of stock Picta

Outdoors

Picta 1.1.2 colours and skies at 1.0.1 brightness, a slight exposure trim, a touch more contrast and glow on clear days, and warm sunlight on sunny summer days.

Indoors

Houses, inns and dungeons each get their own light setting. Less flat fill light, so windows, candles and fires do more of the work, without extra bloom. The starting room is left as Picta made it.

Seasons

No rain from December to February, snow in winter at the old Snowy Holds amount, and no summer snow.

Good to know

Traps I already hit

  • Interiors and the Alternate Perspective start forest look different from Skyrim outdoors. That's by design; Picta doesn't cover them the same way.
  • If you save changes in the in-game Effects 11 menu, the Data copy and the Root twin drift apart. Tell your helper to re-sync the twin afterwards.
  • A Picta update renumbers its records. Every patch that depends on Picta needs checking before you switch versions.
  • Raising ambient light alone makes Picta look darker, because the eye adaptation compensates. Brighten with exposure instead.
  • MO2 drops a disabled mod's plugin from your lists when it opens. For A/B tests keep the mod on and untick only the plugin.

Not included, and why

Depth of field: AMON's shader can't be shared; build it from your own AMON download. PureDark upscaler: paid. Picta 4K assets and ReShade: stock downloads you get yourself.
